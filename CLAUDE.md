# HireHospo Repo Memory

Working repo for HireHospo ops, sales, credit, collections and marketing.
HireHospo is a NZ credit-led finance provider for commercial kitchen equipment;
Washpro sources, delivers, installs and services the gear, HireHospo does the
finance. Detailed references: `docs/BUSINESS_OVERVIEW.md`, the `data/` database,
and the HireHospo skills (`hirehospo-sales`, `hirehospo-products`,
`hirehospo-credit`, `hirehospo-collections`, `hirehospo-ops`).

## Brand quick-reference

**Typography, confirmed from hirehospo.com:**
- Headlines: **Merriweather** (serif, bold). Fallback `Georgia, serif`.
- Body & labels: **Inter**. Fallback `Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif`.
- These are the only two brand typefaces. Do NOT use Space Grotesk or a
  monospace face; those were provisional placeholders in earlier drafts.

**Colours: light navy + gold system, confirmed from the live catalog
(portal.hirehospo.com). Gold is the single accent.**

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| navy | `#1C3D5A` | primary: header bands, headings, primary buttons, links |
| gold | `#F4A62A` | the one warm accent: CTAs, key highlights, "FINANCE", price figures |
| page | `#F4F5F7` | light page background |
| card | `#FFFFFF` | cards / surfaces |
| panel | `#F6F8FA` | soft panels |
| line | `#E5E8EC` | hairlines / borders |
| ink | `#1C3D5A` | headings (navy) |
| body | `#3F4A57` | body text (slate) |
| mute | `#8A94A0` | captions / microcopy |

The brand is light (white / soft-grey), navy-led, with gold as the one accent.
Gold buttons use navy text (as on the site's "Enquiry List" button). Hex values
are read from a screenshot of the live catalog; swap for official codes if
provided. (An earlier provisional dark "service-kitchen" palette with a flame
accent has been retired.)

**Voice:** underwriting, not selling. Clear, confident, structured, zero
pressure. NZ English, restrained punctuation. No jargon (leverage, synergy,
seamless, solutions, etc.). **Avoid em dashes** (use commas, colons, or full
stops instead), especially in customer-facing copy.

**Lead / enquiry auto-replies are sent as human, personal emails** from a named
person (e.g. Urman), plain and first-person, not designed marketing templates.
Urman handles pricing personally and asks leads to browse the brochure and note
the machines they want before the call.

**Finance compliance for customer-facing copy:**
- Never quote a specific weekly/daily price before credit approval. Sell the
  model ("low weekly payments + GST"), not a number.
- "+ GST" on every payment mention.
- Include "subject to credit approval; normal lending criteria apply".
- Approvals framed as "24 to 48 hours", never "instant" or "guaranteed".
- No urgency/pressure language; no discount-shop wording.
- Roles clean: HireHospo does the finance; Washpro does delivery/install/service.
