# HireHospo working repo - standing rules

These are hard rules. They apply to every output: ad copy, captions, scripts,
emails, landing pages, documents, and chat replies. Do not override them from
any other reference file in this repo.

## Writing style

- **No em dashes. Ever.** Not in copy, not in prose, not in chat. Rewrite the
  sentence with a full stop, a comma, a colon, or brackets instead. Do not
  substitute a hyphen or an en dash to get around this.
- NZ English. Plain numbers. Restrained punctuation. Exclamation marks almost
  never.

## Service area

- **Auckland only. "Auckland Wide."** Never write "NZ-wide", "across New
  Zealand", "nationwide", or any national claim in customer-facing copy.
- Geo-target Auckland region in paid media so targeting matches the copy.
- National hashtags (#nzhospitality, #hospitalitynz, #restaurantnz) are out.
  Use Auckland tags.

## Catalogue

- **We do NOT do refrigeration.** No fridges, freezers, chillers, ice machines,
  cool rooms, or display refrigeration in any copy, list, or category mention.
  This overrides the "refrigeration & ice" category listed in
  `docs/BUSINESS_OVERVIEW.md`, which is out of date on this point.
- Safe categories to name: combi ovens, convection ovens, commercial
  dishwashers, glasswashers, deep fryers, ranges and cooktops, griddles, grills
  and salamanders, pizza and conveyor ovens, bakery and dough equipment,
  holding and food warming, food prep and slicing.

## Finance copy (unchanged house rules)

- Every payment or price figure carries **"+ GST"**.
- **"Subject to credit approval"** on anything showing finance terms.
- Sell the payment model, not a specific weekly or daily figure for a specific
  product. Pricing follows credit approval.
- Never: guaranteed or instant approval, no credit checks, pressure or scarcity
  mechanics, discount-shop language, "used" or "second-hand" as apology. The
  phrase is "refurbished, with warranty".
- HireHospo does the finance. Washpro does sourcing, delivery, install and
  service. Do not blur the two.
