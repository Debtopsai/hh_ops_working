# HireHospo — Organic Social Strategy (Instagram + Facebook)

*90-day plan. Cadence: 1 post per day on each platform. Formats: single statics, carousels, videos (reels). Statics and carousels are produced in Poppy.ai (see `02-poppy-ai-static-workflow-prompt.md`). Videos are produced by the video producer, scripted through `/meta-ad-hook-writer` then `/hirehospo-ad-factory` or `/meta-ad-script-writer`. Full day-by-day schedule in `01-90-day-content-calendar.md`. All copy follows the HireHospo locks in `CLAUDE.md`. No em dashes.*

---

## 1. The operating model (read this first)

**One core creative per day, cross-posted to both platforms, caption tailored per platform.**
- 7 core creatives per week, not 14. Each is posted to Instagram and Facebook the same day.
- **Instagram caption:** CTA is **"Comment BROCHURE"** (auto-DM the stock list). IG hashtag set.
- **Facebook caption:** CTA is the **direct link, https://portal.hirehospo.com/brochure**, with the locked FB hashtag set (`#hirehospo #hosponz #AucklandHospitality #HospitalityNZ #nzhospitality #nzcafe #nzrestaurant #nzhotel #hotelnz`).
- Post the vertical/portrait creative to both (4:5 for feed, 9:16 for reels/stories). Reels cross-post to FB Reels.

**Why cross-post and not bespoke per platform:** at this cadence, with one operator and one video producer, bespoke-per-platform doubles the workload for little gain. The audiences barely overlap and the caption swap is what matters. Revisit only if a platform clearly wants its own creative.

## 2. Objectives and KPIs

**North-star goal:** brochure / stock-list requests. Everything funnels to the stock list.
- Instagram: **BROCHURE** comment-to-DM conversations.
- Facebook: **link clicks** to `portal.hirehospo.com/brochure`.

**Metrics, in priority order:**
1. Stock-list requests (IG DM triggers + FB link clicks). *The one that matters.*
2. Saves and shares (intent signals, strongest on carousels and how-to content).
3. Reach and follower growth (top of funnel).
4. Video retention / ThruPlays (tells you which hooks hold).
5. Profile visits and DM conversations.

**Review cadence:** quick weekly scan, proper monthly review. Judge by **pillar** (which themes pull) and **format** (static vs carousel vs video), not by single posts. Organic numbers start small; watch the trend and the intent signals (saves, shares, DMs), not vanity reach.

## 4. Content pillars

Nine pillars, drawn from the brand and the existing ad work. Each pillar has a home day (Section 5).

1. **Value / price** — from $4.66/day +GST, from $6.99/day +GST, minimal upfront cost, low weekly payments.
2. **Product spotlight** — real catalogue equipment (Rational, Starline, Turbofan, Convotherm, Waldorf, Blue Seal, glasswashers, fryers, bakery, refrigeration).
3. **Education / how it works** — apply, rent vs lease, GST, approval, warranty, what you can finance.
4. **Social proof** — top-hospo angle, testimonials, delivered-and-installed montages.
5. **Speed / urgency** — apply in 5 minutes, decision in 1 to 2 business days, delivered fast.
6. **Capital preservation / smart money** — you don't need to buy a $50,000 oven, keep your cash working, lease to own.
7. **Sustainability** — recondition and redeploy, efficient equipment, less waste (per `CLAUDE.md` + About Us mission).
8. **Brand / community** — financing futures, Auckland-based, with you every step.
9. **Industry segments** — "What do Auckland's top [cafés / restaurants / bakeries / bars / caterers / hotels / takeaways / food trucks / venues] have in common?" (ties to `hirehospo-meta-launch/06`).

## 5. The weekly rhythm (the repeatable skeleton)

Same shape every week so production batches cleanly and the audience learns the rhythm. Topics change weekly (calendar in `01`).

| Day | Pillar | Format | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Value / price | Static | Start the week with the offer, quick to produce |
| **Tue** | Product spotlight | Video (reel) | Equipment moves best in motion |
| **Wed** | Education / how it works | Carousel | Mid-week, save-worthy, teaches |
| **Thu** | Social proof | Video (reel) | Proof pulls hardest as video |
| **Fri** | Speed / urgency | Static | End-of-week nudge, quick to produce |
| **Sat** | Sustainability or Brand (alternate) | Carousel | Weekend, softer, story-led |
| **Sun** | Industry "Top Hospo" | Video (reel) | Self-selects the operator, strong hook |

**Weekly format mix: 3 videos, 2 carousels, 2 statics.**
- Videos: Tue, Thu, Sun.
- Carousels: Wed, Sat.
- Statics: Mon, Fri.

**Lean fallback if video production is tight:** drop to **2 videos/week** (keep Tue + Sun, make Thu a carousel or static). That is 24 reels over the quarter instead of 36. Decide this up front with the producer.

## 6. Monthly themes (the 3-month arc)

Today is mid-August (NZ winter). The quarter runs into spring and the lead-up to the summer and Christmas trade, so Month 3 leans seasonal.

- **Month 1 (Weeks 1 to 4) — Foundation: "Meet HireHospo and the offer."** Who we are, the core value ($4.66/day +GST), how finance works, rent vs lease, the range. Build the base.
- **Month 2 (Weeks 5 to 8) — Proof and industries.** One industry focus per week (cafés, restaurants, bakeries + bars, hotels + caterers). Testimonials, delivered installs, product depth. Show it working everywhere.
- **Month 3 (Weeks 9 to 12) — Act now + sustainability + summer/Christmas prep.** Seasonal urgency ("get summer-ready", "be ready for December service"), the sustainability mission in full, and a hard push to the stock list.

## 7. Production workflow

**Batch a month at a time.** In the last week of each month, produce the next month's 28 posts (about 12 videos, 8 carousels, 8 statics).

**Videos (hooks + scripts).** The hook and script skills live in the Claude session, not in Poppy and not on the producer's machine, so there are two paths. Use A by default.
- **Path A (recommended):** generate hooks and scripts **in this Claude session** in the monthly batch, using `/meta-ad-hook-writer` then `/hirehospo-ad-factory` (full package) or `/meta-ad-script-writer` (script only). Save the scripts to the repo and hand them to the producer. The producer needs no skills.
- **Path B (portable):** when generating outside the session (in Poppy or any chat tool), use the self-contained Hook + Script prompt in `03-video-hook-and-script-prompt.md`, which replicates the same method with the HireHospo locks baked in.

Then:
1. Producer shoots/edits in the house style (bold white text, amber emphasis, real catalogue equipment, HireHospo end card with "GET OUR LATEST STOCK LIST TODAY").
2. `/hirehospo-reel-caption` — write the IG + FB captions (Mode A from the finished cut, or Mode B to pre-write from the script).

**Statics + carousels (Poppy.ai):**
1. Take the day's brief from the calendar (pillar + message + any price/equipment).
2. Run it through the Poppy.ai workflow (`02-poppy-ai-static-workflow-prompt.md`) to get the on-image copy, slide breakdown, image direction, and both captions.
3. Finalise the visual in Poppy against the brand kit.

**Scheduling:** load everything into Meta Business Suite Planner and schedule both platforms for the month. Keep 2 to 3 days of buffer so nothing posts empty.

## 8. Compliance and voice locks (every post)

Full detail in `CLAUDE.md`. The non-negotiables:
- **No em dashes.** Warm, confident, direct voice.
- **"kitchen equipment" / "equipment"**, never "gear", "kit", "kit out".
- **"+GST" only on prices HireHospo quotes** ($4.66/day +GST, $6.99/day +GST). Never on rhetorical figures ("a $50,000 oven", "a $20,000 combi").
- **"Subject to credit approval"** on any finance claim. Approval never implied guaranteed. Timeframe 1 to 2 business days, most under $25k.
- Real catalogue equipment and brands only. Auckland, NZD, NZ English.
- No Washpro in customer-facing copy. Refurb stays low-key, except sustainability posts where "recondition and redeploy" is a fair, low-key virtue. Keep green claims truthful and substantiatable.
- CTA: "Get our latest stock list today" → IG Comment BROCHURE, FB link.

## 9. Risks and honest notes

- **3 videos/week is the main resourcing risk.** 36 reels in a quarter is a lot for one producer. Use the template system (swap on-screen text over kitchen/equipment footage, like the existing cuts) to keep each cut cheap, or take the lean 2-video fallback.
- **Comment BROCHURE depends on the auto-reply being live.** Set up ManyChat or Meta's native auto-reply before Week 1, or the IG CTA collects comments with no delivery.
- **Organic reach is slow to start.** The paid Meta launch (`hirehospo-meta-launch/`) and this organic plan should run together: paid buys reach now, organic builds the owned audience and retargeting pools.
- **This is a repeatable system, not a fixed script.** The weekly skeleton and pillars stay; swap topics freely as stock, seasons and results dictate.
