# HireHospo — 90-Day Content Calendar (Instagram + Facebook)

*12 weeks, 1 post per day per platform, cross-posted (one core creative, caption tailored per platform). Weekly skeleton: Mon static (value) · Tue video (product) · Wed carousel (education) · Thu video (social proof) · Fri static (speed) · Sat carousel (sustainability/brand) · Sun video (industry). Format mix per week: 3 video, 2 carousel, 2 static. See `00-organic-social-strategy.md`.*

**Global caption rule (applies to every post below, not repeated per row):**
- **Instagram:** end on **"Comment BROCHURE and we'll send you our latest stock list."** + IG hashtag set.
- **Facebook:** end on **"Get our latest stock list today: https://portal.hirehospo.com/brochure"** + locked FB set (`#hirehospo #hosponz #AucklandHospitality #HospitalityNZ #nzhospitality #nzcafe #nzrestaurant #nzhotel #hotelnz`).
- Finance posts carry **"Subject to credit approval."** "+GST" only on real quoted prices.

**Video pipeline note:** every video row = `/meta-ad-hook-writer` → `/hirehospo-ad-factory` or `/meta-ad-script-writer` → produce → `/hirehospo-reel-caption`. **Static/carousel rows** = Poppy.ai workflow (`02`).
