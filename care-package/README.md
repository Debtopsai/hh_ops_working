# Equipment Care Package

A care guide handed to every customer after they sign, so they understand that
commercial kitchen equipment needs real maintenance. Built in response to a
pattern seen on lead calls and confirmed by the Washpro tech team: customers
treat these machines like domestic appliances, skip maintenance, and then blame
the equipment when it fails. Nearly all of those failures are preventable.

## What's in the guide

1. Why care matters (commercial gear is not domestic; warranty covers faults, not
   neglect; downtime costs more than maintenance).
2. The five rules that prevent most breakdowns (water/scale, daily cleaning,
   airflow, correct use, scheduled servicing).
3. A maintenance rhythm table (daily / weekly / monthly / quarterly / yearly) with
   the heavy-use rule: ~8 hours a day means a professional service about 4 times a
   year.
4. Machine-by-machine quick care for all catalogue categories, each with the "what
   kills them" failure to avoid.
5. A kitchen-environment checklist (ventilation, coil airflow, ambient heat, water,
   cleanliness, power).
6. Warranty covered vs not covered.
7. Who does what, how to book a service, and a simple service log to fill in.

## What we recommend (the important part)

**A guide educates, but reading alone will not change behaviour.** The root cause
here is that customers do not schedule maintenance for themselves. So pair the
guide with something that makes the maintenance actually happen:

1. **Attach this guide at onboarding** (PDF in the welcome pack, and ideally
   walked through briefly at install by Washpro). This is layer one.
2. **Set up a proactive service cadence.** Two options, in order of impact:
   - **A Washpro Care Plan:** a scheduled service built into the arrangement
     (quarterly for heavy-use sites, once or twice a year for lighter use). This
     removes the customer's need to remember, and it turns servicing into a
     revenue line for Washpro rather than a cost centre.
   - **At minimum, automated service reminders** from the CRM (HubSpot/Zoho) or
     ThriveDesk, on a quarterly cadence, with one-tap booking. Much cheaper to set
     up, still a big improvement on nothing.
3. **A one-page "quick care" summary** to print and pin up in the kitchen, where
   the team actually is. We can produce this as a companion to the full guide.
4. **A short care card per machine** that Washpro leaves with each delivered unit
   (the relevant machine-by-machine section, printed as a small card).

Why it pays back: fewer breakdowns, longer equipment life, fewer warranty
disputes (a documented care record makes the neglect-vs-fault question clear), and
a better customer experience. For a finance business carrying the equipment, every
avoided failure protects the asset and the relationship.

## Content and compliance notes

- Voice is HireHospo's: clear, confident, practical, NZ English, no jargon, no em
  dashes. Honest that care is required, but not alarmist.
- The guide does not overstate the warranty. It states plainly what is covered
  (genuine faults) and what is not (neglect and misuse), which protects HireHospo
  in a dispute.
- Roles stay clean: HireHospo finances, Washpro services, the customer does daily
