# HireHospo reel caption — output template

Produce the blocks below in order. Never use em dashes anywhere.

## 1. Transcript (Mode A) or Reel script (Mode B)
- **Mode A (transcribe):** state this is the on-screen text read from the video (audio is
  not transcribable in this environment). Give the joined-up script as one quoted line,
  then the beat-by-beat sequence. Note video length, aspect ratio, any real catalogue
  equipment visible, and the end card.
- **Mode B (build):** give the editor-ready reel script as a table:
  `t | On-screen text (amber = emphasis) | Visual`, opening on a hook and closing on the
  HireHospo end card.

## 2. Instagram caption
Structure:
- **Hook line** (echo or sharpen the video's/reel's opening line). One emoji max.
- **Body** (2 to 3 short lines): the offer in brand voice. Approved phrases:
  "low weekly payments", "minimal upfront cost", "pays for itself from day one",
  "keep your cash working". Say "kitchen equipment", never "gear".
- **CTA line:** "Comment BROCHURE and we'll send you our latest stock list. 👇"
- **Microcopy line:** *Subject to credit approval.*
- **Hashtag block** (8 to 12): mix brand, category, and Auckland/NZ hospo tags.

## 3. Facebook caption
Same story, tuned for Facebook:
- Same hook and body, but Facebook tolerates a little more text, so a slightly fuller
  middle is fine.
- **CTA line:** a direct link, "👉 Get our latest stock list today: [your stock-list link]"
  (Facebook is link-friendly; Comment BROCHURE is Instagram-native, do not use it here).
- **Microcopy line:** Finance is subject to credit approval.
- **Hashtag block (use this exact set, best post volume on Facebook):**
  `#hirehospo #hosponz #AucklandHospitality #HospitalityNZ #nzhospitality #nzcafe #nzrestaurant #nzhotel #hotelnz`

## 4. Notes
- Explain why the two captions differ (IG Comment BROCHURE auto-DM vs FB direct link).
- Flag any figure decisions: "+GST" attaches only to prices HireHospo quotes
  (e.g. $4.66/day +GST), never to rhetorical figures (e.g. "a $50,000 oven").
- Remind that "Comment BROCHURE" only works if the Meta/ManyChat auto-reply is set up to
  DM the stock-list link. Offer to write that auto-DM message.
- Remind to drop the real URL into the Facebook `[your stock-list link]` placeholder.
- Keep any sustainability claim truthful and substantiatable (NZ Commerce Commission).
- Ranges use "1 to 2 days" to stay dash-free; an en dash "1–2 days" is acceptable for a
  pure numeric range (it is not an em dash), but the spelled-out form is safest.
