# Audio brief - HireHospo "Friday, Seven O'Clock" - 15s

## Music brief

- **Genre/feel:** warm, capable, mid-tempo - understated groove, muted electric bass, brushed percussion, a little more forward motion than the sibling ads because this ad has a clock in it. **No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.**
- **BPM 104**, steady, no tempo change. Feel: a bar at full tilt, heard from the back room. Competent, busy, not frantic.
- **Key:** minor through the problem beats, resolving to relative major at the bridge. The resolution should feel like relief, not victory.
- **Energy curve by beat:**
  | Time | Beat | Energy | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:00-0:02 | Timestamp | 2/10 | Almost nothing. Bass pulse and the muffled room. The stillness sets up the clock. |
  | 0:02-0:03.5 | Dead machine | 2/10 | Hold. **Do not lift here** - the flatness is the point. |
  | 0:03.5-0:05.5 | Glass count | 5/10 | Percussion enters with the counter. Rising, but mechanical rather than dramatic. |
  | 0:05.5-0:07 | Hand-wash | **1/10** | **Near-total drop** on "hand-wash". Water takes the mix. |
  | 0:07-0:09 | Bridge | 6/10 | Full arrangement returns on the wordmark. The lift is the arrangement re-entering, not a volume push. |
  | 0:09-0:13 | Proof + figure | 6/10 | Hold steady. A small warm pad under the $4.66 beat - warmth, not sparkle. |
  | 0:13-0:15 | End card | 5/10 → resolve | Settle and resolve, 1.5s tail past the last frame for platform trim. |
- **Search terms for the library:** "busy understated groove 104bpm", "warm analogue bass mid-tempo", "documentary business bed with drive". Avoid uplifting, inspiring, motivational, epic, and anything with a build-and-drop shape.

## VO direction - credit-led operator

- **Voice:** clear, confident, structured. **NZ accent.** Mid-range, slightly warm, unhurried. **~2.1 wps** on this script - this is the sparsest of the three, and the space is intentional.
- **Character note for the booth:** you have been called about a dead glasswasher on a Friday before. You are not alarmed and you are not selling. You are describing a situation the listener is already in, then telling them what can be done about it. Calm competence is the entire performance.
- **Zero hype, zero upsell energy.** No rising terminals. Clean breath cuts, trimmed to −30dB or lower.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03.5 | "Friday, seven o'clock. The glasswasher just died." | **Two separate statements, not one sentence.** Full stop and a real 0.5s pause after "o'clock". "The glasswasher just died" is delivered completely flat - no drama, no sympathy. It is a fact about a Friday. |
| 0:03.5-0:07 | "Every glass in the place is now a hand-wash." | Slightly slower than the hook. Small stress on **"every"**. The line ends but the counter keeps going - **leave the tail dry, no trailing warmth**, so the silence after it feels like the night continuing. |
| 0:07-0:09 | "It's on our stock list." | The only line that gains warmth. Stress **"stock list"**. Steady, no lift at the end. The brand is on screen, not in this line; do not add it back. |
| 0:09-0:13 | "Fully serviced, with warranty. From $4.66 a day." | **The most important read in the ad.** "Fully serviced" must carry the same confidence as "with warranty" - not a hedge, not an apology. Then a small pause before the figure. No GST line in this read - see the claim check in the script. |
| 0:13-0:15 | "Get our latest stock list today." | An instruction delivered calmly. Full stop. No rise, no smile. |

- Record **3 reads of the hook** (flatter / slightly warmer / with a longer pause after "o'clock") and **2 reads of the $4.66 line** (neutral and half a degree warmer) for the editor.
- Record an alternate take of the $4.66 line with the figure spoken as "four dollars sixty-six a day" in case the client prefers the longhand.

## SFX - mapped to storyboard timestamps

| Timestamp | Cue | Purpose | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Muffled bar room tone (voices, glass, a door) heard through a wall | Establish Friday night without showing a person | −22dB |
| 0:00.6 | Single clock-colon tick, then nothing | The clock that stops - sets the ad's whole premise in one sound | −20dB |
| 0:02 | **Absence cue:** room tone continues, but the machine-cycle hum that should be there is missing | The dead machine, told by what is not in the mix. Print a version *with* the hum for A/B - the contrast sells it | (−) |
| 0:03.5 | Tap running, steady; count-up tick underneath (~30 ticks, not decelerating) | The hand-wash reality; the tick that does not stop | −16dB (tap), −18dB (tick) |
| 0:05.5 | Water fills the mix; music near-drops | The agitation peak | −12dB (water) |
| 0:07 | **Water cuts dead** + clean UI tick on APPROVED + soft steel swell on the wordmark | The bridge. The audio reset lands on the same frame as the visual reset - this is the single most important edit in the ad | −12dB (tick), −20dB (swell) |
| 0:09 | Single dish-rack clack, then a short wash-cycle hum starting up | The machine running again - the payoff to the 0:02 absence cue | −16dB (clack), −18dB (hum) |
| 0:11 | Stamp settle (short, dry, low thud + light metallic ring) on `FULLY SERVICED · WITH WARRANTY` | The objection-killer beat | −14dB |
| 0:11.7 | Till / receipt tick on the `$4.66/day` reveal | The money beat | −16dB |
| 0:13.2 | Button (soft, single, no click-tail) on the CTA pill | CTA | −16dB |

**SFX discipline:** the water and the missing-then-returning wash-cycle hum are this ad's entire kitchen-sound budget. One rack clack. Nothing else. Resist adding glass clinks - the ad is about glasses not being washed.

## Ducking & loudness

- Music **−6 to −9 dB under VO**; the hook may ride unducked. The water at 0:05.5 is the one cue permitted to sit **above** the music bed.
- **VO is the priority track.** Sidechain the bed off the VO, 40ms attack / 220ms release.
- **Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP.**
- **Silent-read requirement:** the ad must carry fully with sound off. Burned-in captions plus on-screen text deliver every claim, including `SUBJECT TO CREDIT APPROVAL`. The audio reset at 0:07 has a matching visual reset precisely so the muted version loses nothing structural.
