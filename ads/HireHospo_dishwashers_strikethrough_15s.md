## ⚠ Two things to confirm before this runs

**1. $12,000 is not a real listing.** The dishwasher catalogue runs $2,895–$27,995, and there is nothing at $12,000. The nearest real machine is the **Starline PW1 Heavy Duty Pot/Dishwasher at $11,195** — round that to "$11,195" and the number is catalogue-true, or keep $12,000 and accept it's an illustrative figure rather than a price anyone can point at.

Worth knowing either way: **$12,000 is the heavy-duty / pot-washer tier, not a cafe dishwasher.** A cafe or bar sees $12,000 and assumes the ad isn't for them. If the target is the mainstream operator, the honest strike-through is **$4,195** (Hobart FX-90A undercounter). If the target is restaurants and hotels doing volume, $12,000 is right.

**2. The strike-through implies the crossed price is available at the daily rate.** Crossing out $12,000 and replacing it with $8.99/day reads as *"this machine, for $8.99 a day"* — "FROM" doesn't undo what the viewer just watched happen. The two figures need to describe the same machine, or the ad compares the ceiling to the floor. Either match them, or strike a range (`$2,895–$12,000`) so the daily rate plainly attaches to the bottom.

Also: **$8.99/day is now the sixth daily rate in circulation** ($4.66 brand reference, $3.99 and $6.99 live, $14.99 in Video_01, $11.99 in the oven cut). Which is right is your call, but they can't all run at once.
