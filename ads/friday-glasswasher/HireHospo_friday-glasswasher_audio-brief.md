- BPM 96, steady. Feel: a good bar at 10pm. Busy but under control.
- Energy curve: restrained hook (0-3s, kick out, bed only) → dip on the capital-hit (3-7s, bass drops out, filtered) → lift at the bridge (0:07, full groove returns on the downbeat) → settle on the $4.66 / approval beats (10-13.5s) → resolve on the tonic + 1s tail on the end card.
