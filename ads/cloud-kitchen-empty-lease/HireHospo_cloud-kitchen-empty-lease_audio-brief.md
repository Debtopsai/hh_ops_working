# Audio brief - HireHospo "Lease Signed, Kitchen Empty" - 15s

## Music brief
- Genre/feel: warm, capable, mid-tempo understated groove. No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.
- BPM 94, steady. Feel: a quiet kitchen just before the first order lands. Patient, then in motion.
- Energy curve: restrained hook (bed only) → dip on the problem beat (filtered, kick out) → lift at the bridge (0:07, full groove on the downbeat) → settle on the proof → resolve on the tonic + 1s tail on the end card.
- Library search terms: "minimal warm electronic 94 bpm no vocals", "build-up groove restrained confident", "late-night kitchen instrumental".

## VO direction - credit-led operator
- Voice: clear, confident, structured. NZ accent. ~1.8 wps (ceiling 2.6). Zero hype, zero upsell. A lender who doesn't need the deal, talking to an operator who knows their numbers. Clean breath cuts.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:02.5 | "Lease signed. Kitchen still empty?" | Two short statements, then a genuine question. Let the room breathe. |
| 0:02.5-0:07 | "No fryers, no griddle, no orders. And the rent's still due." | Even rhythm on the three "no"s; "rent's still due" drier, lower. |
| 0:07-0:10 | "HireHospo. Approved in 24 to 48 hours." | Lift. Small beat after "HireHospo". Say "twenty-four to forty-eight". |
| 0:10-0:13 | "Delivered in 1 to 3 business days." | Settled, factual. Don't rush; the visual carries "after deposit". |
| 0:13-0:15 | "Apply now." | Warm, final. The credit line is on screen. |

- Record 2-3 reads of the hook and the CTA at varying warmth for the editor.
- Pronunciation: "HireHospo" = HIRE-HOSS-po.
