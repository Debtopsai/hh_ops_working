- **Why this is the stronger of the two headlines:** "$14.99/day" is an abstraction a viewer has to convert before it means anything. "A coffee and a muffin" is already converted - it is a price they know in their body, and for a hospitality operator it is a price they *charge*. The headline does the arithmetic for the viewer in four words, which is why line 2 gets the longest single beat in the ad and the image is allowed to sit still underneath it.
- **Audit:** headline verbatim and first ✓ · single CTA ✓ · 35 words / 2.33 wps (max beat 2.50 wps) ✓ · specifics: $50,000, $14.99/day, four real categories, "24 to 48 hours", fully-serviced-with-warranty ✓ · "Subject to credit approval" in the disclosure block **and** on the end card ✓ · catalogue-true ✓ (no single model named) · roles clean ✓ · no approval hype, pressure, or discount language ✓.
- **⚠ Claim check (two items now):**
  1. **No GST line, by client direction.** NZ B2B ads may quote ex-GST, but only if they say so - a $14.99/day figure that silently excludes GST is a misleading price representation under the Fair Trading Act. **The clean fix is to make the figure GST-inclusive**, after which no GST line is needed. Credit to confirm which route.
  2. `$14.99/day` is not on the approved claims table and **needs Credit sign-off before this runs**. On the current book it reconciles to roughly $7,400-$8,200 of equipment rather than $50,000. The disclosure block is the fix for the range pairing; the figure itself is Credit's call. It is a single parameterised token, so a change is a one-line edit.
  - **Never state the price of the coffee and muffin on screen.** The headline makes the comparison; putting a dollar figure on the food would be a claim about third-party retail pricing HireHospo has no basis to make. The equivalence is the viewer's own.
  - No single product model is named. A model beside a daily rate reads as a per-product quote and breaches the credit gate.
- **Shorter-cut trim (10s):** keep both headline lines and the image whole - the reframe is the ad and it does not compress. Cut the category tiles entirely and shorten the proof to 2s. Beat map: headline L1 0-2.5 · headline L2 2.5-6 · HireHospo 6-7 · proof + figure 7-9 · CTA 9-10. The disclosure block must survive every trim.
