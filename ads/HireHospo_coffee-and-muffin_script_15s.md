### ⚠ Claim check

1. **`$14.99/day` is not on the approved claims table and needs Credit sign-off.** At 36-month Lease-to-Own, $14.99/day = $104.93/week = **$16,369 over 156 weeks**. Against the **2.0-2.2× multiple observed across real HireHospo Lease-to-Own contracts**, that funds roughly **$7,400-$8,200 of equipment**. *(Internal figures - they size the hook, they never appear in creative.)*
2. **Which means this ad's arithmetic actually works.** A cafe starter package - undercounter dishwasher plus a convection oven, or a dishwasher plus a glasswasher - lands in the **$7,000-$8,500** range on the real category bands. **$14.99/day is a defensible entry rate for exactly the equipment this ad shows.** That is the difference between this cut and `50k-from-1499`: the same figure, attached to the right size of package. Recommend Credit is asked to sign off **this** pairing first.
3. **The word "package" is doing compliance work and must not be cut.** The on-screen disclosure reads `PACKAGE RATE`, not a product rate. No single product model is named anywhere in this ad, and none may be added - a model beside a rate reads as a per-product quote and breaches the credit gate.
4. **"You sell this about fifteen times before noon"** is an observation about cafe trade, not a claim about HireHospo or about any customer's revenue. Keep it visually generic. ⚠ Worth a client sanity-check that fifteen is a fair morning for the cafes being targeted - if it reads high, "about ten" works identically and the ad loses nothing.
5. **The coffee-and-muffin price is never stated on screen**, and must not be. The ad implies the equivalence through the lit tile; stating "a coffee and a muffin costs $14.99" would be a claim about third-party retail pricing that HireHospo has no basis to make.
6. Category price bands are a **26 May 2026 snapshot** - ⚠ verify on hirehospo.com before paid use.
- **Shorter-cut trim (10s):** keep the hook and the interrupt whole - together they are the entire concept and neither survives compression. Cut the category relight to 1.5s and shorten the end card to 2s. Beat map: hook 0-3.5 · interrupt 3.5-6 · bridge 6-7 · proof 7-9 · CTA 9-10. The disclosure block must survive every trim.
