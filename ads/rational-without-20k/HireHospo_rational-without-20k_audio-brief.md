# Audio brief - HireHospo "Rational Without the $20K" - 15s

## Music brief
- Genre/feel: warm, capable, mid-tempo understated groove (muted Rhodes, round bass, soft kick, light shaker). No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.
- BPM 100, steady. Feel: a restaurant kitchen after the last ticket. Competent, calm, a little warmth.
- Energy curve: restrained hook (0-3s, bed only) → dip on the capital hit (3-7s, low-pass filter, kick out) → lift at the bridge (0:07, full groove on the downbeat) → settle on the ledger ticks (10-12.5s) → resolve on the tonic + 1s tail on the end card.
- Library search terms: "warm neo-soul instrumental 100 bpm no vocals", "understated confident groove minimal", "lounge rhodes groove restrained".

## VO direction - credit-led operator
- Voice: clear, confident, structured. NZ accent. ~2.2-2.5 wps. Zero hype, zero upsell. A lender who doesn't need the deal, talking to a restaurant owner who knows their numbers. Clean breath cuts.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | "Paying cash for a combi oven?" | Genuine question, mild curiosity, not a gotcha. Light stress on "cash". |
| 0:03-0:07 | "That's twenty grand not paying wages, or stock." | Flat and factual. Stress "twenty grand". Small pause before "or stock". |
| 0:07-0:10 | "HireHospo finances a refurbished Rational, with warranty." | Brighter, steady. "Refurbished" said with confidence. Tiny beat before "with warranty". |
| 0:10-0:12.5 | "Low weekly payments. Your capital stays put." | Two short, settled statements. Land "stays put" low and certain. |
| 0:12.5-0:15 | "Apply now. Subject to credit approval." | Calm close. "Apply now" warm; the credit line as matter-of-fact as a receipt. |

- Record 2-3 reads of the hook and the CTA at varying warmth for the editor.
- Pronunciation: "HireHospo" = HIRE-HOSS-po; "Rational" = RASH-un-al (the brand is said like the English word).
