## Script - "Sitting In A Quote" - Restaurant / caterer scaling - Product Aware - High-stakes warning - PAS - 15s

**Offer focus:** Lease-to-Own (36 months) · **Featured gear:** multi-category fit-out - Combi Ovens, Commercial Dishwashers, Ranges, Food Prep (real categories, **no single model named**) · **HireHospo intro at:** 0:07.5 (50%) · **CTA:** Get our latest stock list today
**Format:** person-free motion graphics + real equipment imagery (dark). **Voice:** credit-led operator - underwriting, not selling.
**Compliance:** ✅ **ZERO unapproved claims. This ad can be produced and published today** without waiting on Credit. Every claim is on the approved table: "Up to $50,000" · "low weekly payments" · "Fully serviced, with warranty" · "Approved in 24 to 48 hours" · "Subject to credit approval". **No daily or weekly figure appears anywhere**, by design - which is also what removes the ceiling-and-floor juxtaposition risk.

**Role in the set:** this is the **control** for the two figure-led cuts (`50k-from-1499`, `coffee-and-muffin`). It tests the same $50,000 ceiling with no rate attached, answering the question those ads cannot: does the funding ceiling sell on its own, or is the daily figure doing the work? If Credit rejects `$14.99/day`, this ad still runs.

- **Bridge intent honoured:** the hook puts the equipment and the obstacle in the same frame - the gear is not unaffordable, it is *stationary*, sitting in a document. The agitation names the reason without arguing with it, so there is nothing for the viewer to defend against. That leaves "Up to fifty thousand dollars" as the only thing in the ad that moves the number out of the paper and into the kitchen, which is exactly what the category tiles then do on screen - and because the brand is carried by the wordmark rather than named in the line, nothing in the VO sounds like it is selling.

- **⚠ Claim check:**
