**Headline (supplied, leads the ad):** *"$50k worth of equipment from $14.99/day"*
**Offer focus:** Lease-to-Own (36 months) · **Featured gear:** multi-category fit-out - Combi Ovens, Commercial Dishwashers, Ranges, Food Prep (real categories, **no single model named**) · **CTA:** Apply now

**Structure note:** this is a **Most Aware, offer-led** cut, so the offer *is* the hook - claim → proof → CTA. The "offer lands last" rule governs Problem-Aware ads; it does not apply here.
**Compliance:** ⚠ the `$14.99/day` figure needs Credit sign-off (see below). The disclosure block is mandatory and runs under the headline from frame one.

- **Why the headline can carry the whole ad:** a Most Aware viewer does not need the problem explained to them - they need the offer stated plainly and then made credible. So the headline runs first and stays on screen, the category tiles show what "$50,000 of equipment" actually means in real catalogue terms, and "refurbished, with warranty" answers the only question the price raises. Nothing is withheld and nothing is teased.
- **Audit:** headline verbatim and first ✓ · single CTA ✓ · 29 words / 1.93 wps (max beat 2.60 wps) ✓ · specifics: $50,000, $14.99/day, four real categories, "24 to 48 hours", refurbished-with-warranty ✓ · "+ GST" spoken **and** on screen ✓ · "Subject to credit approval" in the disclosure block **and** on the end card ✓ · catalogue-true ✓ (no single model named - a model beside a rate would read as a per-product quote) · roles clean ✓ · no approval hype, pressure, or discount language ✓.
- **⚠ Claim check:** `$14.99/day` is not on the approved claims table and **needs Credit sign-off before this runs**. On the current book it reconciles to roughly $7,400-$8,200 of equipment rather than $50,000, and the headline pairs the top of the funding range with the bottom of the payment range - which is what the disclosure block exists to correct. If Credit sets a different rate, it is a one-line change: the figure is a single parameterised token. Everything else in the ad stands.
- **Shorter-cut trim (10s):** keep both headline lines and the disclosure whole - they are the ad. Cut the category tiles to 1.5s and the proof to 2s. Beat map: headline 0-4 · tiles 4-5.5 · HireHospo 5.5-6.5 · proof 6.5-8.5 · CTA 8.5-10.
