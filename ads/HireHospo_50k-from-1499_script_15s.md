### ⚠ Claim check - BLOCKING

1. **`$14.99/day` is not on the approved claims table and does not reconcile with the book.** At 36-month Lease-to-Own, $14.99/day = $104.93/week = **$16,369 collected over 156 weeks**. Against the **2.0-2.2× multiple observed across real HireHospo Lease-to-Own contracts** (Turbofan E31D4 $41.30/wk; Starline M2 $50-$70/wk; Rational SCC WE101 $189.17/wk), that funds roughly **$7,400-$8,200 of equipment - not $50,000**. Going the other way, **$50,000 of equipment implies roughly $91-$100/day.** The two numbers in this hook are about 6.5× apart.
   *(The multiples above are internal portfolio figures. They size the hook - they never appear in creative.)*
2. **The construction itself is the second risk, independent of the arithmetic.** "$50,000 ... from $14.99/day" pairs the **top of the funding range with the bottom of the payment range**. Under the Fair Trading Act this reads as misleading even where both figures are individually true, because the headline implies they describe the same deal. **The range disclosure block above is the minimum fix** and it must be on screen, legible, and locked to the figure - not a footnote that scrolls past.
3. **Three ways forward, in order of preference:**
   - **(a) Correct the figure.** Keep "$50,000" and let Credit set the true entry rate for a $50k package (~$91-$100/day on current multiples). Loses the coffee-and-muffin magic; gains a hook that survives scrutiny.
   - **(b) Correct the equipment value.** Keep "$14.99/day" and drop the ceiling - the honest pairing is roughly **$7,500-$8,000 of equipment**. This is what `coffee-and-muffin` does, and it is the stronger ad.
   - **(c) Ship the control instead.** `fifty-thousand` uses the approved "Up to $50,000" claim with no daily figure at all and can run today.
4. **Never name a specific product beside the daily figure.** The category grid shows categories only. A model name next to a rate reads as a per-product quote and breaches the credit gate.
5. Category price bands are a **26 May 2026 snapshot** - ⚠ verify on hirehospo.com before paid use.
- **Shorter-cut trim (10s):** keep the hook and the figure-leaving-frame beat (together they are the argument), cut the category relight to 1.5s, keep the bridge and end card intact. **The range disclosure block must survive every trim** - if it will not fit legibly, the cut cannot run.
