# Video script — OVENS — 15s

**Format:** house format, matched to `Video_01_revision.mp4` — real kitchen footage, two-line text lockups, **music bed only, no voiceover**, person-free until the end card.
**Canvas:** 1080×1920, 9:16, 30fps *(Video_01 mastered at 1906px — use 1920 here)*
**ICP:** restaurant owner / caterer · **Awareness:** Problem Aware → Solution Aware · **Offer:** Lease-to-Own 36 months
**Hero anchor:** Rational SCC101 10 Tray Electric Combi Oven, **$20,000** ([product page](https://hirehospo.com/products/rational-scc101-10-tray-electric-combi-oven)) — a real, active, exactly-$20,000 listing
**CTA:** Get Stock List *(matching the live creative — not "Apply now")*

**Why this build:** the count-up-to-a-big-number hook is the account's cheapest lead source ("50k equipment", **$7.41 CPL**). This keeps that hook and points it at one category. Approval speed is kept off the front — the "Quick Process" ad returned **0 leads on 1,178 impressions** — and demoted to end-card reassurance.

## Music

No VO. Warm, capable, mid-tempo bed, **96–104 BPM**, understated groove — a well-run kitchen after service. No vocals, no EDM drop, no inspirational piano. Energy: restrained under the count-up, small lift on the cut at 0:02.5, hold flat through the panel stack, settle from 0:12.5 with a short tail. Master **−14 LUFS, −1.0 dBTP**. Optional SFX, all subtle: a ticking counter under the count-up, one oven-door thunk at 0:08.0, a soft UI click as the button lands.

### ⚠ Confirm before publishing

1. **Refurbished vs new.** All 76 oven records in the Meta product feed carry `condition: "new"`. This script says only "WITH WARRANTY" and never claims refurbished — deliberately safe either way. If the feed is wrong and stock is refurbished, beat 6 can become white "REFURBISHED" / gold "WITH WARRANTY", which is the stronger line.
2. **GST basis.** Confirm catalogue prices are GST-exclusive before "$20,000" runs with a `+ GST` tag. If they are inclusive, the tag reads `incl. GST`.
3. **If you want a daily figure in beat 2** — the live video uses "FROM $14.99/DAY", the brand reference says $4.66/day, and the account runs $3.99 and $6.99. **Four numbers.** Settle which one is signed off first; the beat works without one.
4. **Prices are a snapshot** — Meta product catalog, 4 Sep 2026. Re-verify $20,000 on the live product page before launch.
