# Audio brief - HireHospo "Get The Glasswasher You Really Want" - 16s

**Inherits the SCC101 comparison mix** (`HireHospo_machines-you-really-want_audio-brief.md`). Same voice, same structure, same ducking and loudness. Only the deltas below change. **Record all four comparison ads in one session** - the family only works if the voice and read are identical across them.

## Music - delta

- **BPM 106** (SCC101 runs 108, dishwashers 100, ovens 104). Feel: **a bar at opening, not mid-service** - a little brighter and more forward than the siblings, because this is the cheapest tier and the decision is a small one.
- Same bed family, same arrangement, same energy shape: restrained through the table, **one step up at 0:09 when the table clears**, percussion pulls out under the hero line, resolve on the end card with a 1.5s tail.
- **The lift at 0:09 is the whole mix.** Everything before it is rhythm under a table; everything after is one sentence with room around it.

## VO - the lines that differ

NZ accent, credit-led operator, ~2.1 wps, zero hype. **Do not perform the hero line** - the empty frame does that work.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:01 | "Same glasswasher." | Flat. Two words, full stop. The comparison rests on the viewer accepting this, so state it, do not sell it. |
| 0:02.5-0:04 | "Thirty-two hundred today." | Even and factual. **Do not stress the figure.** It is the smallest number in the family and over-emphasising it makes the ad sound like it is straining. |
| 0:04-0:06 | "Or four sixty-six a day." | **"Or" and "from" both matter.** Articulate "four sixty-six" cleanly - it is the only approved rate in the whole campaign and it has to be unambiguous. Slightly warmer than the previous line, but only slightly. |
| 0:09-0:11.5 | "Get the glasswasher you really want." | **The line the ad exists for. Underplay it.** No emphasis on "really", no rise, no smile. Record four takes, flat to flattest, take the flattest that is still warm. |
| 0:11.5-0:13.5 | "Without the thirty-two hundred." | Quieter than the hero line. The strike-through does the emphasis. **Also record the Variant B line: "Fixed before the weekend."** - same register, no extra urgency in the voice, the words carry it. |
| 0:13.5-0:16 | "Get our latest stock list today." | Calm instruction. Full stop, no rise. |

**Record both longhand and shorthand of the rate** ("four sixty-six" and "four dollars sixty-six") plus a `[RATE]` gap take, even though this figure is approved - it keeps the session consistent with the sibling ads.

## SFX - delta

| Timestamp | Cue | Level |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Glass-rack rattle, light and dry** - this ad's signature sound, and the one thing that tells the viewer which category they are in before any text lands. Keep it short; a long rattle reads as breakage, which is the wrong association | −16dB |
| 0:02.5 / 0:04 | Till tick on each figure, the second warmer than the first | −16dB |
| 0:06 → 0:09 | Row ticks, **four pairs, 750ms apart**. Rows 1 and 4 are double ticks (both sides). Rows 2 and 3 are a dry tick left, a warm tick right - the audio carries the comparison even muted | −18dB |
| 0:09 | **Nothing.** No SFX under the hero line | - |
| 0:11.5 | Strike on the struck-through figure, then two soft ticks for the benefit chips | −14dB / −16dB |
| 0:13.5 | Button on the CTA pill | −16dB |

**Discipline:** one category sound at 0:00, then the mix is entirely money and UI. **No bar atmosphere, no glass clinks, no crowd** - this is a comparison card, not a scene, and the sibling ads all hold the same line.

## Ducking & loudness

Unchanged from the SCC101 brief: music −6 to −9 dB under VO, sidechain 40ms/220ms, master **−14 LUFS / −1.0 dBTP**.

**Silent-read requirement:** the table, both figures, both outcome lines and the full disclaimer are all on screen. The muted cut carries the entire argument.
