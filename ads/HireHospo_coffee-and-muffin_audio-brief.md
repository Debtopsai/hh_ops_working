# Audio brief - HireHospo "One Of Them Pays For The Kitchen" - 15s

**⚠ The `$14.99/day` figure appears in one spoken line (0:09-0:13) and needs Credit sign-off.** Record the alternate pickup listed below in the same session so the ad can be recut without a second booking.

## Music brief

- **Genre/feel:** warm, capable, mid-tempo - the most *human* of the set, because the hook is a cafe counter rather than a balance sheet. Muted electric bass, brushed percussion, a little warmth in the top end. **No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.**
- **BPM 100**, steady. Feel: a good cafe at half past nine - busy, unhurried, competent.
- **Key:** major-leaning throughout. **This is the one ad in the set that does not need a minor section** - nothing has gone wrong in it. The structure is recognition, not rescue.
- **Energy curve by beat:**
  | Time | Beat | Energy | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:00-0:01.5 | The image | 3/10 | Bass, room tone, nothing else. **Do not score the image.** The viewer needs to recognise an object, not be told how to feel about it. |
  | 0:01.5-0:04 | The counter | 4/10 | Percussion enters with the ticks and builds gently across the count to fifteen. |
  | 0:04-0:05.5 | Fifteen tiles | **1/10** | **Near-total drop.** The ticks stop dead. This silence is where the viewer does the arithmetic - protect it. |
  | 0:05.5-0:07.5 | One lights | 3/10 | A single low sub hit on the flame, then a warm pad only. Still restrained. |
  | 0:07.5-0:09 | Bridge | 6/10 | Full arrangement returns on the wordmark. |
  | 0:09-0:13 | Proof + figure | 6/10 | Hold. Warm and steady. |
  | 0:13-0:15 | End card | 5/10 → resolve | Settle, resolve, 1.5s tail past the last frame. |
- **Search terms:** "warm cafe groove 100bpm", "bright understated bass", "friendly minimal business bed". Avoid uplifting, inspiring, motivational, epic - and avoid anything with a coffee-shop-jazz cliché, which will date the ad instantly.

## VO direction - credit-led operator

- **Voice:** clear, confident, structured. **NZ accent.** Mid-range, warm - **the warmest read in the set**, though still nowhere near salesy. **~2.1 wps.**
- **Character note for the booth:** you are not explaining anything. You are pointing at something the listener already knows and letting them notice what it means. The read is closer to an observation made across a counter than to a piece of advertising. **If it sounds like a reveal, it is wrong** - the viewer should feel they worked it out, not that they were told.
- **Zero hype.** No rising terminals. Clean breath cuts, trimmed to −30dB or lower.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:04 | "You sell this about fifteen times before noon." | Conversational and plain. Small stress on **"this"** at the top - it points at the image. **"About fifteen" must sound approximate**, not precise; the hedge is what keeps it honest and it also makes the line feel observed rather than researched. No lift at the end. |
| 0:04-0:07.5 | "One of them pays for the kitchen." | **The line the whole ad rests on, and it must be underplayed.** Quieter than the hook, slightly slower, completely flat. No emphasis anywhere - not on "one", not on "kitchen". The tile lighting does the pointing; the voice must not also point, or the moment becomes a sales line. **A real 0.6s of silence before it**, held from the previous beat. |
| 0:07.5-0:09 | "HireHospo finances it." | Steady, matter-of-fact. Stress nothing. This is the shortest bridge line in the set and it should pass almost unnoticed - by this point the viewer is already looking for it. |
| 0:09-0:13 | "Refurbished equipment, with warranty. From $14.99 a day plus GST." | "Refurbished" carries the same confidence as "with warranty" - **never apologetic**. Small pause before the figure. **"From" is load-bearing** and must be clearly articulated - it is what keeps the rate a starting point rather than a quote. **"Plus GST" spoken at full level**, never swallowed. |
| 0:13-0:15 | "Apply now." | Calm instruction. Full stop, no rise, no smile. |

- Record **3 reads of "One of them pays for the kitchen"** - flat / flatter / flattest. Take the flattest one that is still audible. Directors reliably over-perform this line; having a deliberately underplayed take in the bin protects the ad from itself.
- **Record the alternate pickup:** "Refurbished equipment, with warranty. Low weekly payments, plus GST." - the figure-free version, in case Credit rejects the rate.
- Record an alternate hook with **"about ten times before noon"** in case the client wants the more conservative number.

## SFX - mapped to storyboard timestamps

| Timestamp | Cue | Purpose | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Cafe room tone, distant and muffled (machine hiss, cups, low voices) - **no distinct words** | Establish the world without a person in frame | −24dB |
| 0:01.5 | Counter tick per increment, ×15, 130ms apart, even and mechanical | The count. Deliberately machine-like against the warm room tone - it is a ledger counting a menu item | −18dB |
| 0:04 | **Ticks stop dead. Room tone drops to a low sustain** | The arithmetic beat. Silence is the instrument | (drop) |
| 0:05.5 | A single low sub hit as the one tile lights | The interrupt, marked exactly once | −18dB |
| 0:07.5 | Clean UI tick on APPROVED + soft steel swell on the wordmark | The bridge | −12dB (tick), −20dB (swell) |
| 0:09 | Three tile ticks, warm, 150ms apart + a single dish-rack clack | The equipment beat; the clack is the only kitchen sound in this ad | −18dB (ticks), −16dB (clack) |
| 0:10.7 | Stamp settle on `REFURBISHED · WITH WARRANTY` | The objection-killer beat | −14dB |
| 0:11.7 | Till / receipt tick on the figure reveal | The money beat - and a deliberate callback to a cafe till, which is where the ad started | −16dB |
| 0:13.2 | Button on the CTA pill | CTA | −16dB |

**SFX discipline:** the counter tick and the till tick are the same sound family on purpose - the ad opens on a cafe counting sales and closes on a cafe counting money. Do not add coffee-machine steam, cup clinks, or milk frothing; the room tone already carries the location and literal foley would tip it into pastiche.

## Ducking & loudness

- Music **−6 to −9 dB under VO**; the hook rides unducked (the bed is at 3/10 and the VO is sparse).
- **Protect the 0:04-0:05.5 silence.** Do not let the limiter or any bus compressor pull room tone up into it - gate it if necessary. That 1.5 seconds is the most valuable audio real estate in the ad.
- **VO is the priority track.** Sidechain the bed off the VO, 40ms attack / 220ms release.
- **Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP.**
- **Silent-read requirement:** the ad must carry fully with sound off - and this one does so better than any other in the set, because the concept is a photograph and one tile changing colour. Burned-in captions plus on-screen text deliver every claim, including `PACKAGE RATE`, `+ GST` and `SUBJECT TO CREDIT APPROVAL`.
