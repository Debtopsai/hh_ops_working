# Audio brief - HireHospo "$50k Of Equipment For A Coffee & Muffin A Day" - 15s

**Headline (supplied):** *"$50k worth of equipment for the price of a coffee & muffin a day."* - spoken across the first two beats, verbatim.
**⚠ Do not book VO until Credit has signed off the `$14.99/day` figure.** It appears in one spoken line (0:11-0:13). Record the alternate pickup below in the same session.

## Music brief

- **Genre/feel:** warm, capable, mid-tempo - the warmest bed in the set, because the headline is built on something people enjoy. Muted electric bass, brushed percussion, a little warmth in the top end. **No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano - and no coffee-shop-jazz cliché**, which would date the ad instantly.
- **BPM 100**, steady.
- **Key:** major throughout. **No minor section** - this is an offer, not a problem.
- **Energy curve by beat:**
  | Time | Beat | Energy | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:00-0:03 | Headline L1 | 4/10 | Bass and a tick. Confident from the first bar. |
  | 0:03-0:05 | The image | 4/10 | **Hold - do not score the image.** Room tone does the work. The viewer needs to recognise an object, not be told how to feel about it. |
  | 0:05-0:07.5 | Headline L2 | 5/10 | One step up as the reframe lands. **A step, not a swell** - the line is doing the work. |
  | 0:07.5-0:09.5 | HireHospo | 6/10 | Full arrangement on the wordmark. |
  | 0:09.5-0:13 | Tiles + proof + figure | 6/10 | Hold, warm and steady. |
  | 0:13-0:15 | End card | 5/10 → resolve | Settle, resolve, 1.5s tail past the last frame. |
- **Search terms:** "warm groove 100bpm", "bright understated bass", "friendly minimal business bed". Avoid uplifting, inspiring, motivational, epic, and anything tagged cafe or coffeehouse.

## VO direction - credit-led operator

- **Voice:** clear, confident, structured. **NZ accent.** Mid-range, warm - **the warmest read in the set**, though still nowhere near salesy. **~2.1 wps.**
- **Character note:** you are making a comparison, not a joke. The coffee-and-muffin line is the most quotable thing in the whole ad library and it will be tempting to land it with a wink. **Don't.** Say it exactly as flatly as you said the fifty thousand - the contrast between the two halves is the entire effect, and a knowing delivery collapses it.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | "Fifty thousand dollars' worth of equipment." | Even and factual. Small stress on **"equipment"**. Full stop, then a real **0.5s pause** while the image comes up. The pause is where the viewer starts wondering what the second half is. |
| 0:03-0:07.5 | "For the price of a coffee and a muffin a day." | **The line the ad rests on - deliver it completely straight.** Same pitch, same warmth, same pace as the first line. No stress on "coffee", none on "muffin", none on "day". No smile. It is a price comparison stated as a fact, and stating it flatly is what makes it land as true rather than as a slogan. |
| 0:07.5-0:09.5 | "All on our stock list." | Steady, matter-of-fact. Stress **"stock list"**. No lift. The brand is on screen, not in this line; do not add it back. |
| 0:09.5-0:12.5 | "Refurbished, with warranty. From $14.99 a day." | "Refurbished" carries the same confidence as "with warranty" - **never apologetic**. **"From" is load-bearing** and must be clearly articulated. "+ GST" is no longer spoken here; it is burned into the disclosure block beneath the figure, which satisfies the requirement. |
| 0:12.5-0:15 | "Get our latest stock list today." | Calm and even. Small stress on **"stock list"**. Full stop, no rise, no smile. |

- Record **4 reads of the coffee-and-muffin line** - flat / flatter / flattest / one deliberately warm. Take the flattest one that is still engaging. Directors reliably over-perform this line; having an underplayed take in the bin protects the ad from itself.
- **Record the alternate figure pickup:** "From [RATE] a day, plus GST." with the figure as a separate punch-in, so a change from Credit does not need a second session.

## SFX - mapped to storyboard timestamps

| Timestamp | Cue | Purpose | Level |

| 0:00 | Single ledger tick as the headline locks | Mark the number | −18dB |
| 0:03 | Cafe room tone fades in, distant and muffled - **no distinct words** + a soft cup-set as the image resolves | Locate the comparison without a person in frame. The cup-set is the single most important small sound in the ad - dry, close, one cup meeting one saucer | −24dB (tone), −18dB (cup) |
| 0:05 | Room tone holds under the headline line 2 | The comparison sits in its own world | −24dB |
| 0:07.5 | **Room tone cuts dead** + clean UI tick on APPROVED + soft steel swell on the wordmark | The reset. The audio and visual resets land on the same frame - this is the most important edit in the ad | −12dB (tick), −20dB (swell) |
| 0:09.5 | Four tile ticks, 150ms apart, warm | What the money actually buys | −18dB |
| 0:11.2 | Single dish-rack clack + stamp settle on `REFURBISHED · WITH WARRANTY` | Kitchen texture (once) and the objection-killer beat | −16dB / −14dB |
| 0:12.2 | Till / receipt tick on the figure | The money beat - a deliberate callback to the cup-set at 0:03, same sound family | −16dB |
| 0:13.2 | Button on the CTA pill | CTA | −16dB |

**SFX discipline:** the cup-set at 0:03 and the till tick at 0:12.2 are a matched pair and they are the ad's whole sound design. Do not add steam, frothing, or cup clinks - the room tone already carries the location and literal foley tips it into pastiche.

## Ducking & loudness

- Music **−6 to −9 dB under VO**; the headline may ride unducked (the bed is at 4/10 and the VO is sparse).
- **VO is the priority track.** Sidechain the bed off the VO, 40ms attack / 220ms release.
- **Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP.**
- **Silent-read requirement:** the ad carries fully muted - the headline is on screen for its full seven and a half seconds and the disclosure block is burned in beneath the figure. Captions plus on-screen text deliver every claim, including `+ GST` and `SUBJECT TO CREDIT APPROVAL`.
