Rows 1 and 4 match on both sides. Conceding half the rows is what makes the card read as analysis rather than as a sales sweep.

- **Why the outcome lines are dishwasher-specific:** the reason an operator upgrades a warewasher is never the machine, it is the **labour and the bottleneck**. `STAFF ON THE FLOOR, NOT AT THE SINK` is the real outcome and it is the strongest line in any of the three ads, because it converts a capital decision into a staffing one. `NO PILE-UP AT THE PASS` is the felt version of the same thing. Neither claims a throughput figure, which is deliberate - **never quote racks per hour or cycle times**, those are spec claims that live on the product page.

### ⚠ Claim check

1. **`$7.86/day` is derived** from the real portfolio weekly for a Starline M2 on 36-month Lease-to-Own (**$55.00/wk ÷ 7**). Portfolio rows for this unit range **$50 to $70/wk**, so the true "from" figure may sit lower - **$50/wk would be $7.14/day.** ⚠ **The portal brochure figure governs.** Parameterise it.
2. **`$8,730` is derived** using the ratio confirmed by the SCC101 pair (buy ÷ 1,111 = daily). Inside the real Commercial Dishwashers band ($2,000-$20,000), but an estimate until read off the portal.
3. **The Starline M2 is a pass-through/hood machine**, not an undercounter. Match the product photo and the audience to that - this ad is for restaurants, bars and high-volume kitchens, **not** for a small cafe. A cafe-facing dishwasher ad wants an undercounter unit and its own figures.
4. **`OWN IT AT END OF TERM` on the right is not negotiable.** Ownership transfers at end of the 36-month term.
5. **`8.7K OUT OF YOUR POCKET` on the left, never `8.7K DEBT`.**
6. **`TAX DEDUCTIBLE*`** keeps its asterisk and disclaimer. ⚠ Accountant sign-off, or drop row 4.
7. **Never quote racks per hour, cycle time, capacity or water use.** Specs live on the product page and vary by unit. The outcome lines deliberately describe the kitchen, not the machine.
8. **No GST line, by client direction** - same Fair Trading note as the sibling ads.
- **Shorter-cut trim (8s):** cuts 1, 3, 5, 9, 11. VO: "Same dishwasher. Eight and a half thousand today, or seven eighty-six a day. Staff on the floor, not at the sink. Get our latest stock list today." **Keep the staff line in every cut** - it is the strongest thing in the ad.
