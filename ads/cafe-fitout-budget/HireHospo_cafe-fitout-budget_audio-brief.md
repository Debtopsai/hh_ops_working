# Audio brief - HireHospo "Fit-Out Bigger Than the Budget" - 15s

## Music brief
- Genre/feel: warm, capable, mid-tempo understated groove. No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.
- BPM 98, steady. Feel: a cafe at 6am before opening. Quiet, warm, ready.
- Energy curve: restrained hook (bed only) → dip on the problem beat (filtered, kick out) → lift at the bridge (0:07, full groove on the downbeat) → settle on the proof → resolve on the tonic + 1s tail on the end card.
- Library search terms: "warm acoustic-electronic groove 98 bpm no vocals", "morning cafe instrumental understated", "lo-fi warm keys confident".

## VO direction - credit-led operator
- Voice: clear, confident, structured. NZ accent. ~2.3 wps (ceiling 2.6). Zero hype, zero upsell. A lender who doesn't need the deal, talking to an operator who knows their numbers. Clean breath cuts.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | "Fit-out quote bigger than your budget?" | Knowing, a little wry. Stress "bigger". |
| 0:03-0:07 | "Dishwasher, oven, fryer. One cheque before your first coffee." | List the three flat, one beat each. Land "first coffee" lower. |
| 0:07-0:10 | "HireHospo finances it. Up to $50,000." | Brighter, steady. Small pause before "Up to". Say "fifty thousand", not "fifty K". |
| 0:10-0:12.5 | "Refurbished, with warranty. Low weekly payments." | Confident on "refurbished". Two settled statements. |
| 0:12.5-0:15 | "Apply now. Subject to credit approval." | Calm close; the credit line plain, like a receipt. |

- Record 2-3 reads of the hook and the CTA at varying warmth for the editor.
- Pronunciation: "HireHospo" = HIRE-HOSS-po.
