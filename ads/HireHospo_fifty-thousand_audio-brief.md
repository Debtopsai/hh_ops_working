# Audio brief - HireHospo "Sitting In A Quote" - 15s

**✅ No claim in this script needs sign-off.** Book the VO session whenever you like - and if you are recording the other two cuts, record this one in the same session, since it is the one guaranteed to run.

## Music brief

- **Genre/feel:** warm, capable, mid-tempo - understated groove, muted electric bass, brushed percussion. Restrained, like the rest of the set. **No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.**
- **BPM 96**, steady, no tempo change. Feel: a kitchen that is ready to be built and is waiting on a decision.
- **Key:** minor through the quote beats, resolving to relative major at the bridge and staying there.
- **Energy curve by beat:**
  | Time | Beat | Energy | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:00-0:02 | The number | 4/10 | Bass and a single tick. Present, unhurried. |
  | 0:02-0:04 | The quote closes | 3/10 | **Energy drops as the plate desaturates.** The arrangement thins in step with the colour leaving. |
  | 0:04-0:06 | Dark kitchen | 2/10 | Low service ambience only. Something is missing from the mix, matching the unlit tiles. |
  | 0:06-0:07.5 | "Because of the number" | **1/10** | **Near-total drop.** Low sustain only. |
  | 0:07.5-0:10 | Bridge | 6/10 | Full arrangement returns as the paper clears. |
  | 0:10-0:13.5 | Proof + model | 6/10 | Hold. Warm pad under the tile relight. |
  | 0:13.5-0:15 | End card | 5/10 → resolve | Settle, resolve, 1.5s tail past the last frame. |
- **Search terms:** "understated corporate groove 96bpm", "warm analogue mid-tempo", "documentary business bed". Avoid uplifting, inspiring, motivational, epic.

## VO direction - credit-led operator

- **Voice:** clear, confident, structured. **NZ accent.** Mid-range, slightly warm, unhurried. **~2.2 wps.**
- **Character note for the booth:** the equipment in this ad is not unaffordable - it is *stationary*. Nothing is wrong, nothing is urgent, a decision simply has not been made. Read it as someone describing a situation accurately, with no opinion about it. The absence of concern in the voice is what makes the ad feel like a lender rather than a salesperson.
- **Zero hype.** No rising terminals. Clean breath cuts, trimmed to −30dB or lower.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:04 | "Fifty thousand dollars of equipment. Sitting in a quote." | Flat and factual. Full stop after "equipment", then a real 0.4s pause. **"Sitting in a quote" is the important half** - deliver it slightly slower and slightly quieter, with a small stress on **"sitting"**. That word is the entire premise: the gear exists, it just is not moving. |
| 0:04-0:07.5 | "Not in your kitchen. Because of the number." | Two flat statements. **No sympathy in the read** - the moment it sounds sympathetic it becomes a sales line. A real pause between them. "Because of the number" is the quietest phrase in the ad, almost thrown away. |
| 0:07.5-0:10 | "Up to fifty thousand dollars." | The only line that gains warmth. **"Up to" must be clearly articulated** - do not let it run together as "upto"; it is the approved wording and the whole claim rests on it. Steady, no lift at the end. The brand is on screen, not in this line. |
| 0:10-0:12 | "Fully serviced. With warranty." | "Fully serviced" carries the same confidence as "with warranty" - **never apologetic**. Closed statement, full stop. "+ GST" is burned in on the payment-model frame and the end card rather than spoken. |
| 0:12-0:15 | "Get our latest stock list today." | Calm and even, six words across three seconds - unhurried. Small stress on **"stock list"**. Full stop, no rise, no smile. |

- Record **3 reads of the hook** (flatter / slightly warmer / with a longer pause before "Sitting in a quote") and **2 reads of the CTA**.
- **Do not record any take containing a daily or weekly figure.** There is no version of this ad that carries one - that is the point of it.

## SFX - mapped to storyboard timestamps

| Timestamp | Cue | Purpose | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Single ledger tick as the figure locks | Establish; mark the number | −18dB |
| 0:02 | Paper-settle - dry, close, a single sheet coming to rest on a hard surface | The quote closing around the number. **This is the ad's signature sound** and it should feel small and final, not dramatic | −16dB |
| 0:04 | Low service ambience enters, distant (extractor, faint clatter) | The kitchen that has not happened yet | −24dB |
| 0:06 | **Ambience and music drop to a low sustain** | The "because of the number" beat | (drop) |
| 0:07.5 | Paper clears - a single upward sweep + clean UI tick on APPROVED + soft steel swell on the wordmark | The bridge. The paper leaving is the release the ad has been holding since 0:02 | −15dB (sweep), −12dB (tick), −20dB (swell) |
| 0:10 | Four tile ticks, warm, 150ms apart + a single dish-rack clack | The kitchen happening; the clack is the only kitchen sound in this ad | −18dB (ticks), −16dB (clack) |
| 0:11.7 | Stamp settle on `FULLY SERVICED · WITH WARRANTY` | The objection-killer beat | −14dB |
| 0:12.7 | Till / receipt tick on the payment-model line | The money-model beat | −16dB |
| 0:13.7 | Button on the CTA pill | CTA | −16dB |

**SFX discipline:** the paper-settle at 0:02 and the paper-clear at 0:07.5 are a matched pair and the whole sound design hangs off them. Use the same source recording for both, reversed and swept for the clear. One rack clack. Nothing else from the kitchen world.

## Ducking & loudness

- Music **−6 to −9 dB under VO**; the hook may ride unducked. SFX peaks duck a further **3-6 dB**.
- **VO is the priority track.** Sidechain the bed off the VO, 40ms attack / 220ms release.
- **Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP.**
- **Silent-read requirement:** the ad must carry fully with sound off. Burned-in captions plus on-screen text deliver every claim, including `FUNDING UP TO $50,000`, `+ GST` and `SUBJECT TO CREDIT APPROVAL`. The quote-closing and paper-clearing beats are visual first and audio second, so nothing structural is lost muted.
