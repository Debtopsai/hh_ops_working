### Beat 3 — the comparison (the signature beat)

Three stacked panels. **No weekly or daily figure appears in any of them** — that is what keeps this compliant, and it is also what makes it read fast.

| Panel | Label (white) | Value | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BUY IT OUTRIGHT | **$4,195 + GST, TODAY** | Footage **desaturated to ~30%**, value in white, not gold — this is the option you are arguing against |
| 2 | RENT — 12 MONTHS | **LOW WEEKLY PAYMENTS** | Full colour, value in `#FDC100` |
| 3 | LEASE-TO-OWN — 36 MONTHS | **YOURS AT THE END** | Full colour, value in `#FDC100` |

The desaturation on panel 1 is the whole argument — the eye goes to the two panels that are lit. Don't add a red cross or a strike-through; the colour drop does the work and stays out of comparative-advertising territory.

Footage per panel: 1 — a till/EFTPOS terminal or an invoice on a stainless bench. 2 — undercounter unit mid-cycle. 3 — the same machine, wide, settled in a finished kitchen.

## Music

No VO. Same bed family as the ovens cut so the two read as a set: warm, capable, **96–104 BPM**, no vocals. Energy: restrained under the count-up, lift at 0:02.5, a small three-step rise as the comparison panels stack (one accent per panel), settle from 0:12.5. Master **−14 LUFS, −1.0 dBTP**. Optional SFX: counter tick under the hook, a soft water/steam swell as the hood lifts at 0:00, one rack-clack per comparison panel, UI click on the button.

### ⚠ Confirm before publishing

1. **Refurbished vs new.** All 44 dishwasher records in the Meta product feed carry `condition: "new"` while brand guidance says most stock is refurbished-with-warranty. This script claims only "WITH WARRANTY" — safe under either. If refurbished is correct, beat 6 becomes white "REFURBISHED" / gold "WITH WARRANTY".
2. **GST basis** — confirm catalogue prices are GST-exclusive before the `+ GST` tag runs.
3. **Panel 3 wording.** "YOURS AT THE END" is a plain-English rendering of the Lease-to-Own term. Have whoever signs the finance documents confirm it matches the agreement, since it appears on screen as a promise.
4. **No daily figure used.** Dishwashers sit outside the categories a "from $X/day" hook is approved for, and four different daily rates are currently in circulation across the brand reference, the live account and `Video_01`. Left out on purpose.
5. **Prices are a snapshot** — Meta product catalog, 4 Sep 2026. Re-verify $4,195 before launch.
