# Audio brief - HireHospo "Up To Fifty Thousand" - 15s

**⚠ Do not book a VO session until Credit has signed off the `$14.99/day` figure.** The rate appears in one spoken line (0:09-0:13); if it changes, that line is re-recorded. Everything else in this brief holds regardless.

## Music brief

- **Genre/feel:** warm, capable, mid-tempo - understated groove, muted electric bass, brushed percussion. This ad talks to an operator scaling up, so the bed should sound like capability, not aspiration. **No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.**
- **BPM 98**, steady, no tempo change. Feel: a kitchen that already works, being asked to work bigger.
- **Key:** minor through the agitation, resolving to relative major at the bridge.
- **Energy curve by beat:**
  | Time | Beat | Energy | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:00-0:02 | The ceiling number | 4/10 | Bass and a tick. Slightly more forward than the sibling ads - this hook is an opportunity, not a problem. |
  | 0:02-0:04 | Category grid | 5/10 | Percussion enters as the tiles light. Building, but evenly. |
  | 0:04-0:05.5 | The figure leaves | 3/10 | **Energy drops as the number moves.** The arrangement thins in step with the translate. |
  | 0:05.5-0:07 | Empty kitchen | **1/10** | **Near-total drop.** Low sustain only. The emptiness is the point in the mix as much as the frame. |
  | 0:07-0:09 | Bridge | 6/10 | Full arrangement returns on the wordmark. |
  | 0:09-0:13 | Proof + figure | 6/10 | Hold. Warm pad under the relight. |
  | 0:13-0:15 | End card | 5/10 → resolve | Settle, resolve, 1.5s tail past the last frame. |
- **Search terms:** "capable understated groove 98bpm", "warm analogue business bed", "confident minimal bass". Avoid uplifting, inspiring, motivational, epic.

## VO direction - credit-led operator

- **Voice:** clear, confident, structured. **NZ accent.** Mid-range, slightly warm, unhurried. **~2.2 wps.**
- **Character note for the booth:** you are talking to an operator who is expanding, not struggling. Nothing in this read should sound like rescue. It is a capability being described to someone who can use it.
- **Zero hype, zero upsell energy.** No rising terminals. Clean breath cuts, trimmed to −30dB or lower.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:04 | "Fifty thousand dollars of kitchen equipment. Everything on the list." | Even and factual. **Do not sell the number** - it is a quantity, not a prize. Small stress on **"everything"**, then a full stop. "Everything on the list" is said slightly warmer, as though reading it back to them. |
| 0:04-0:07 | "Or fifty thousand out of the business." | The pivot. **Same pitch, same volume, same pace as the previous line** - the sentence does the work, the read must not signal it. A real 0.3s pause before "out of the business". |
| 0:07-0:09 | "HireHospo funds it instead." | The only line that gains warmth. Stress **"instead"**, not the brand name. Steady, no lift at the end. |
| 0:09-0:13 | "Refurbished, with warranty. Packages from $14.99 a day plus GST." | "Refurbished" carries the same confidence as "with warranty" - **never apologetic**. Then a small pause. **"Packages from" is load-bearing and must be clearly articulated** - it is what stops the sentence claiming that $50,000 costs $14.99 a day. Do not let the read run "packagesfrom" together. **"Plus GST" spoken at full level**, never swallowed. |
| 0:13-0:15 | "Apply now." | Calm instruction. Full stop, no rise. |

- Record **3 reads of the hook** and **2 reads of the $14.99 line** (one with a heavier stress on "Packages from").
- **Record a pickup line for option (a):** "Refurbished, with warranty. Low weekly payments, plus GST." - the figure-free version, so the ad can be recut without a second session if Credit rejects the rate.

## SFX - mapped to storyboard timestamps

| Timestamp | Cue | Purpose | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Count-up tick (fine mechanical, decelerating) + low service ambience | Drive the number | −18dB (tick), −24dB (ambience) |
| 0:02 | Four soft tile ticks, 150ms apart, cool-toned | The list being read | −20dB |
| 0:04 | Single ledger tick as the figure begins to move | The money leaving | −20dB |
| 0:05.5 | **Ambience and music duck to a low sustain** | The empty-kitchen beat | (drop) |
| 0:07 | Clean UI tick on APPROVED + soft steel swell on the wordmark | The bridge | −12dB (tick), −20dB (swell) |
| 0:09 | Four tile ticks again, **warmer and slightly closer** than 0:02 | The relight - the same sound, resolved. This rhyme is what sells the reversal | −18dB |
| 0:10.7 | Stamp settle on `REFURBISHED · WITH WARRANTY` | The objection-killer beat | −14dB |
| 0:11.7 | Till / receipt tick on the figure reveal | The money beat | −16dB |
| 0:13.2 | Button on the CTA pill | CTA | −16dB |

**SFX discipline:** the four tile ticks appear exactly twice - cool at 0:02, warm at 0:09. That pairing is the ad's only real sound design. One rack clack is permitted under the relight if the mix feels thin; nothing else from the kitchen world.

## Ducking & loudness

- Music **−6 to −9 dB under VO**; the hook may ride unducked. SFX peaks duck a further **3-6 dB**.
- **VO is the priority track.** Sidechain the bed off the VO, 40ms attack / 220ms release.
- **Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP.**
- **Silent-read requirement:** the ad must carry fully with sound off. **This matters more here than in any other ad in the set** - the range disclosure block is a visual-only compliance element, so the muted version is the version that has to be legally correct. Burned-in captions plus on-screen text deliver every claim, including `+ GST`, `SUBJECT TO CREDIT APPROVAL` and the full three-line disclosure.
