# Audio brief - HireHospo "$50k Of Equipment From $14.99/Day" - 15s

**Headline (supplied):** *"$50k worth of equipment from $14.99/day"* - spoken across the first two beats, verbatim.
**⚠ Do not book VO until Credit has signed off the `$14.99/day` figure.** It appears in one spoken line (0:02-0:05). Record the alternate pickup below in the same session.

## Music brief

- **Genre/feel:** warm, capable, mid-tempo - understated groove, muted electric bass, brushed percussion. This is an offer-led ad to a Most Aware viewer, so the bed should feel like a door already open, not a pitch being made. **No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.**
- **BPM 98**, steady, no tempo change.
- **Key:** major-leaning throughout. **No minor section** - nothing has gone wrong in this ad, there is no problem to agitate. That is what separates the offer-led cuts from the problem-led ones.
- **Energy curve by beat:**
  | Time | Beat | Energy | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:00-0:02 | Headline L1 | 4/10 | Bass and a tick. Confident from the first bar - the ad has nothing to build toward. |
  | 0:02-0:05 | Headline L2 + disclosure | 5/10 | Percussion enters on the figure. Steady, no swell. |
  | 0:05-0:07.5 | Category tiles | 5/10 | Hold. Tile ticks carry the rhythm. |
  | 0:07.5-0:09.5 | HireHospo | 6/10 | Full arrangement on the wordmark. |
  | 0:09.5-0:13 | Proof | 6/10 | Hold, unhurried. |
  | 0:13-0:15 | End card | 5/10 → resolve | Settle, resolve, 1.5s tail past the last frame. |
- **Search terms:** "capable understated groove 98bpm", "warm analogue business bed", "confident minimal bass". Avoid uplifting, inspiring, motivational, epic.

## VO direction - credit-led operator

- **Voice:** clear, confident, structured. **NZ accent.** Mid-range, slightly warm, unhurried. **~1.9 wps** - the sparsest read in the set, because the headline needs room to be read on screen as it is spoken.
- **Character note:** you are stating an offer to someone who is already interested. No persuasion, no build, no reveal. The tone is a lender quoting terms across a desk - the confidence comes from the offer being good, not from how you say it.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:02 | "Fifty thousand dollars' worth of equipment." | Even and factual. **Do not sell the number.** Small stress on **"equipment"** - the noun is the benefit, the figure is just its size. Full stop. |
| 0:02-0:05 | "From fourteen ninety-nine a day, plus GST." | **"From" is load-bearing and must be clearly articulated** - it is what keeps the rate a starting point rather than a quote. Do not let it run into the number. Then the figure, plainly. **"Plus GST" spoken at full level and clarity**, never swallowed or tacked on - it should sound like part of the offer, because it is. |
| 0:05-0:07.5 | "Ovens, dishwashers, ranges, prep." | Four items, evenly spaced, **each one landing with its tile**. This is a list being read, not a sentence - allow a real beat between each. |
| 0:07.5-0:09.5 | "HireHospo funds it." | Steady, matter-of-fact. Stress **"funds"**. Three words, no lift at the end. |
| 0:09.5-0:13 | "Refurbished, with warranty. Approved in 24 to 48 hours." | "Refurbished" carries the same confidence as "with warranty" - **never apologetic**. The approval line is procedural; no warmth needed. |
| 0:13-0:15 | "Apply now." | Calm instruction. Full stop, no rise, no smile. |

- Record **3 reads of the two headline lines** (flat / half a degree warmer / with a longer pause between them) and **2 reads of the CTA**.
- **Record the alternate pickup** in case Credit changes the rate: "From [RATE] a day, plus GST." - leave the figure as a separate punch-in so it can be dropped in without re-recording the line.

## Ducking & loudness

- Music **−6 to −9 dB under VO**. SFX peaks duck a further **3-6 dB**.
- **VO is the priority track.** Sidechain the bed off the VO, 40ms attack / 220ms release.
- **Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP.**
- **Silent-read requirement:** critical here. The headline and the full three-line disclosure are on screen for eleven of the fifteen seconds, so the muted version carries the entire offer and its qualifications without a word of audio. Burned-in captions plus on-screen text deliver every claim.
