# Audio brief - HireHospo "Get The Dishwasher You Really Want" - 16s

**Inherits the SCC101 comparison mix** (`HireHospo_machines-you-really-want_audio-brief.md`). Same voice, same structure, same ducking and loudness. Only the deltas below change. Record all three comparison ads in **one session** - the family only works if the voice and read are identical across them.

## Music - delta

- **BPM 100** (SCC101 runs 108). Feel: busier and lower - a restaurant mid-service.
- Same bed family, same arrangement, same energy shape: restrained through the table, **one step up at 0:09 when the table clears**, percussion pulls out under the hero line, resolve on the end card with a 1.5s tail.
- **The lift at 0:09 is the whole mix.** Everything before it is rhythm under a table; everything after is one sentence with room around it.

## VO - the lines that differ

NZ accent, credit-led operator, ~2.1 wps, zero hype. **Do not perform the hero line** - the emptiness of the frame does that work.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:01 | "Same dishwasher." | Flat. Two words, full stop. The whole comparison rests on the viewer accepting this, so state it, do not sell it. |
| 0:02.5-0:04 | *(the buy price, spoken longhand)* | Even and factual. **Do not stress the figure** - it is the thing the ad argues against, and emphasising it makes the ad sound like it is trying to scare them. |
| 0:04-0:06 | *(the daily rate, spoken longhand)* | **"From" must be clearly articulated** and never run into the number. Slightly warmer than the previous line, but only slightly. |
| 0:09-0:11.5 | "Get the dishwasher you really want." | **The line the ad exists for. Underplay it.** No emphasis on "really", no rise, no smile. It is an observation about what they have been putting off, not a pitch. Record four takes, flat to flattest, and take the flattest that is still warm. |
| 0:11.5-0:13.5 | *(the payoff, "Without the ...")* | Quieter than the hero line. The strike-through does the emphasis. |
| 0:13.5-0:16 | "Get our latest stock list today." | Calm instruction. Full stop, no rise. |

**Record longhand and shorthand of both figures** ("five ninety" and "five dollars ninety"; "eight seven three oh" and "eight thousand seven hundred and thirty") plus a `[RATE]` gap take, so a portal correction does not need a second session.

## SFX - delta

| Timestamp | Cue | Level |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | rack clack, wet and close - **this ad's signature sound**, and the one thing that tells the viewer which category they are in before any text lands | −15dB |
| 0:02.5 / 0:04 | Till tick on each figure, the second warmer than the first | −16dB |
| 0:06 → 0:09 | Row ticks, **four pairs, 750ms apart**. Rows 1 and 4 are double ticks (both sides). Rows 2 and 3 are a dry tick left, a warm tick right - the audio carries the comparison even muted | −18dB |
| 0:09 | **Nothing.** No SFX under the hero line | - |
| 0:11.5 | Strike on the struck-through figure, then two soft ticks for the benefit chips | −14dB / −16dB |
| 0:13.5 | Button on the CTA pill | −16dB |

**Discipline:** one category sound at 0:00, then the mix is entirely money and UI. No kitchen atmosphere - these are comparison cards, not scenes.

## Ducking & loudness

Unchanged from the SCC101 brief: music −6 to −9 dB under VO, sidechain 40ms/220ms, master **−14 LUFS / −1.0 dBTP**.

**Silent-read requirement:** the table, both figures, both outcome lines and the full disclaimer are all on screen. The muted cut carries the entire argument, which is the point - the competitor's ad is silent and this one has to win that comparison too.
