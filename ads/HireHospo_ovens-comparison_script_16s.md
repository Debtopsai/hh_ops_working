Rows 1 and 4 match on both sides. That symmetry is the design - it makes the card read as analysis, so attention lands on rows 2 and 3, which carry the argument.

- **Why the outcome lines are oven-specific:** a convection oven's whole job is even heat, so `CONSISTENT BAKES, EVERY TRAY` is what the machine literally does. `PUT HOT FOOD ON THE MENU` is the cafe's actual ambition and the reason they are looking at all. Neither is a results promise.

### ⚠ Claim check

1. **`$5.90/day` is derived, not published.** It comes from the real portfolio weekly for a Turbofan E31D4 on 36-month Lease-to-Own (**$41.30/wk ÷ 7**). **The portal brochure figure governs** - if it differs, the portal wins. Treat `$5.90` as a parameterised token, not a typed string.
2. **`$6,550` is derived**, using the ratio confirmed by the SCC101 pair (buy ÷ 1,111 = daily). It sits inside the real Convection Ovens band ($2,800-$8,300), but it is **an estimate until someone reads it off the portal.** Do not publish before confirming.
3. **`OWN IT AT END OF TERM` on the right is not negotiable.** Lease-to-Own transfers ownership at end of the 36-month term, never day one.
4. **`6.5K OUT OF YOUR POCKET` on the left, never `6.5K DEBT`.** A cash purchase spends capital; it does not create debt.
5. **`TAX DEDUCTIBLE*`** keeps its asterisk and disclaimer. Treatment depends on the customer's circumstances and differs between the two options. ⚠ Accountant sign-off, or drop row 4 and run three rows.
6. **`CONSISTENT BAKES, EVERY TRAY`** is a capability statement about convection, not a guarantee. Do not escalate to "your baking will be better".
7. **No GST line, by client direction.** Same Fair Trading exposure noted on the other ads: ex-GST is fine in B2B **only where stated**. Cleanest fix is a GST-inclusive figure on the ad and the portal together.
