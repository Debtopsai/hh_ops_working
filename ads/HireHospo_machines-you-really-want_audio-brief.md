# Audio brief - HireHospo "Get The Machines You Really Want" - 16s

**The reference ad we are beating has no audio at all.** That is the single largest and cheapest edge available here, so the mix is not decoration on this one - it is the differentiator. Build it properly.

## Music brief

- **Genre/feel:** warm, capable, mid-tempo with real forward drive. Muted electric bass, tight brushed percussion, a little warmth up top. **No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano.**
- **BPM 108**, steady. Fastest bed in the HireHospo set, because the cut rate is fastest here.
- **Key:** major-leaning throughout. Nothing has gone wrong in this ad. It is a choice being laid out, not a problem being solved.
- **Beat alignment is mandatory.** At 108 BPM a bar is ~2.22s and a beat is ~0.56s. **Every hard cut must land on a beat.** With ten cuts in sixteen seconds, a cut that floats off-grid will read as an error even to a viewer who could not name why.
- **Energy curve:**
  | Time | Beat | Energy | Direction |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0:00-0:02.5 | Hook | 5/10 | Bass and percussion from the first frame. No intro ramp - the ad starts already moving |
  | 0:02.5-0:07 | The comparison | 6/10 | Hold. Let the SFX carry the rhythm of the slams |
  | 0:07-0:09 | Outcome lines | 6/10 | Hold |
  | 0:09-0:11.5 | **Hero line** | **7/10, then strip back** | Lift one step on the cut, then **pull the percussion out** and leave bass and pad under the line. The frame empties and the mix empties with it |
  | 0:11.5-0:13.5 | Payoff | 7/10 | Percussion returns on the strike-through |
  | 0:13.5-0:16 | End card | 5/10 → resolve | Settle, resolve, 1.5s tail past the last frame |
- **Search terms:** "driving understated groove 108bpm", "warm analogue bass with motion", "confident minimal business bed". Avoid uplifting, inspiring, motivational, epic.

## VO direction - credit-led operator

- **Voice:** clear, confident, structured. **NZ accent.** Mid-range, slightly warm. **~1.9 wps** overall - the VO is deliberately sparse against a fast picture, which is what stops the ad feeling frantic.
- **Character note:** the picture is doing the urgency. **The voice must not.** Read it calm and level throughout, including the hero line. A viewer hearing an unhurried voice over a fast cut reads confidence; a viewer hearing a fast voice over a fast cut reads hype, and hype is the one register this brand cannot use.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:01 | "Same oven." | Flat, matter-of-fact. Two words, no colour. It is a statement of fact that sets up the whole comparison |
| 0:01-0:02.5 | "Two ways to pay." | Same level. **No lift on "two"** - do not signal that a comparison is coming, just make it |
| 0:02.5-0:04 | "Thirty thousand today." | Plain. **Do not editorialise the number.** No distaste, no weight. The crosses on screen do that job; if the voice does it too, the comparison reads rigged |
| 0:05-0:07 | "Or twenty-seven a day." | Small warmth, no more. **"Or" is load-bearing** - it is what makes this an alternative rather than a pitch. Articulate it cleanly |
| 0:09-0:11.5 | **"Get the machines you really want."** | **The line the ad exists for.** Slower than everything before it, and quieter. Small stress on **"really"** - that word is the whole line, because it acknowledges they have been settling. No smile, no rise at the end |
| 0:11.5-0:13.5 | "Without the thirty thousand." | Level and final. Delivered as a fact being removed from the table, not as a flourish. Full stop |
| 0:13.5-0:16 | "Get our latest stock list today." | Calm instruction. Small stress on **"stock list"**. No rise, no smile |

- Record **4 reads of the hero line** - level / slightly warmer / slower / one with the stress on "want" instead of "really". Directors reliably over-perform this line; have an underplayed take in the bin.
- Record **2 reads of "Thirty thousand today"** and take the flatter one.

## SFX - mapped to storyboard timestamps

The SFX carry the rhythm in this ad, more than in any other in the set. Every slam is a hit.

| Timestamp | Cue | Purpose | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Low oven-door thunk | Establishes the machine as heavy, expensive, well-made, in one sound. The ad's signature | −15dB |
| 0:01 | Snap ×2, 180ms apart, as the oven splits | The comparison forming | −16dB |
| 0:02.5 | Till tick on `$30,000` | Money beat, neutral tone | −16dB |
| 0:04 | Three dry ticks, 200ms apart, on the crosses | Dry and flat. **Not** ominous - no low hits, no whooshes. The crosses are facts, not threats | −18dB |
| 0:05 | Till tick on `FROM $27.00/DAY`, **warmer than the one at 0:02.5** | Same sound family, different colour. The contrast is the argument, made in audio | −16dB |
| 0:07 / 0:08 | Two soft ticks on the outcome lines | Light, quick | −18dB |
| 0:09 | **Nothing.** Percussion pulls out | The hero line arrives in a hole in the mix. Protect this - no cue, no swell, no riser | (−) |
| 0:11.5 | Strike-through draw | The single most important SFX in the ad. Short, decisive, dry. Not a slash, not a whoosh | −14dB |
| 0:11.7 | Three ticks on the benefit chips, 200ms apart | Warm, matching the 0:05 till tick | −18dB |
| 0:14 | Button on the CTA pill | CTA | −16dB |

**Discipline:** one kitchen sound total, the oven-door thunk at 0:00. Everything else is UI and money. Do not add service ambience - this ad is set nowhere, it is a choice on a screen.

## Ducking & loudness

- Music **−6 to −9 dB under VO**. SFX peaks duck a further **3-6 dB**.
- **The 0:09 hole is not a mix accident and must survive mastering.** Gate it if the limiter tries to pull the bed up into it. That two and a half seconds of near-empty mix under an empty frame is the most valuable real estate in the ad.
- **VO is the priority track.** Sidechain the bed, 40ms attack / 220ms release.
- **Master: −14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP.**
- **Silent-read requirement:** must carry fully muted, since the reference ad is competing on exactly that ground and we have to win it in both states. Burned-in captions plus on-screen text deliver every line, including the hero line, the benefit chips and `SUBJECT TO CREDIT APPROVAL`.
