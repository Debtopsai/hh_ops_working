**Format:** person-free motion graphics + real equipment imagery (dark). **Voice:** credit-led operator - underwriting, not selling.
**Compliance:** approved claims only · **no GST line by client direction (see claim check)** · "Subject to credit approval" on the end card · **no daily figure in this ad** - premium gear leads with capital preservation, never a tiny daily number · no quoted weekly price for a specific product.

That's not the problem." *Promise* - the oven works in the kitchen while the capital keeps working in the business.

- **Bridge intent honoured:** the hook removes the objection the operator would otherwise spend the whole ad defending ("I can't afford it") and replaces it with the one they actually feel but rarely say - that the money is available and spending it is still the wrong move. Once the problem is named as liquidity rather than price, finance stops being a concession and becomes the correct instrument, so "It's on our stock list" needs no argument - it simply points at where the thing already is. The servicing proof then explains why a premium brand is even in the conversation, and the ledger frame states the promise as arithmetic: two outcomes, both ticked. The offer lands last because by then it is the only unstated thing left.
- **Audit:** bridge ✓ (0:09 = 45%) · offer last ✓ (payment model first appears at 0:17) · specifics: $20,000, six months / 26 weeks, Rational SCC WE101, Combi Ovens, "24 to 48 hours", fully-serviced-with-warranty ✓ · single CTA ✓ · 45 words / 2.25 wps (max beat 2.60 wps) ✓ · claims all on the approved table ✓ · credit-approval microcopy ✓ · catalogue-true ✓ · roles clean ✓ · no approval hype, pressure, or discount language ✓.
- **⚠ Claim check:**
  - **"$20,000"** is used as the illustrative cost of a premium combi and sits inside the real Combi Ovens band ($2,700-$33,000). It is the **outright-purchase cost** - the thing the ad argues against - never a HireHospo figure. ⚠ Confirm the band against the live catalogue before paid use.
  - **No daily or weekly figure appears anywhere in this ad, by design.** Premium gear must lead with capital preservation; "$4.66/day" belongs only to the cheapest categories and is deliberately absent here. Do not add one in any cut.
  - **Rational SCC WE101** is a real unit financed in the HireHospo portfolio. ⚠ Confirm it is **active** on the live catalogue before publishing, and link `hirehospo.com/products/<handle>` in the hand-off. If it is not active, substitute another active Rational combi from the catalogue - **do not** fall back to a generic "commercial combi oven".
  - **"Six months"** is a plain-language rendering of the 26-week runway bar, not a claim about any customer's finances. Keep it visually a generic illustration.
  - Lease-to-Own suits operators with ≥1 year trading history. This ad targets established restaurants, so the term framing is correct - ⚠ if it is retargeted to startups, switch the offer focus to Rent (12 months).
