### ⚠ Claim checks — confirm before publishing

1. **Condition — the material one.** All 44 dishwasher records in the Meta product feed carry `condition: "new"`, while brand guidance says most stock is refurbished-with-warranty. The 0:25 line assumes refurbished. **Either** reconcile the feed, **or** swap to: *"With warranty. Serviced by Washpro."* Do not run "refurbished" over stock the feed calls new.
2. **GST basis.** Confirm catalogue prices are GST-exclusive before "$4,195 + GST" airs; if inclusive, the card reads **$4,195 incl. GST**.
3. **Rent end-of-term options** ("purchase at a discount, continue at a reduced rate, or upgrade") are quoted from the offer table. They are contractual terms appearing on screen — have whoever signs off the finance documents confirm the wording matches the agreement.
4. **No daily figure used.** Dishwashers sit outside the categories the "$4.66/day" hook is approved for (glasswashers, hot plates, small fryers), and the live account is running two different numbers ($3.99 and $6.99). Left out on purpose.
5. **Prices are a snapshot** — Meta product catalog, 4 Sep 2026. Re-verify $4,195 on the live product page before launch.
