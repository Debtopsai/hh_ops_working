- **Why the outcome lines are bar-specific:** nobody replaces a glasswasher because they want a glasswasher. They want **Friday night to not fall over**. `CLEAN GLASSES THROUGH A FRIDAY` is the whole job in five words, and `NO HAND-WASHING BEHIND THE BAR` is the failure state every publican has lived. Neither claims a cycle time or a rack count.

### ⚠ Claim check

1. **`From $4.66/day` is on the approved-claims table**, and glasswashers are one of the three categories it is approved for. **No new sign-off needed.** Keep **"from"**; never drop it.
2. **`$3,200` is illustrative**, taken from the middle of the real Glasswashers band ($2,300-$4,000). ⚠ **Confirm against the portal brochure before publishing**, and only ever present it as the outright-purchase cost.
3. **No model name anywhere.** The plinth chip shows the **category**. A model beside a daily rate reads as a per-product quote and breaches the credit gate - this matters more here than on the sibling ads, because this ad carries an actual approved rate.
4. **Do not apply the buy÷1,111 ratio to this tier.** At $4.66/day the multiple over 36 months runs **1.3× to 2.2×** across the band, against **0.98×** on the SCC101. Cheaper gear carries a higher multiple because fixed costs amortise over a smaller asset. The ratio is a rough guide for mid and premium gear only.
5. **`OWN IT AT END OF TERM` on the right is not negotiable.** Ownership transfers at end of the 36-month term.
6. **`3.2K OUT OF YOUR POCKET` on the left, never `3.2K DEBT`.**
7. **`TAX DEDUCTIBLE*`** keeps its asterisk and disclaimer. ⚠ Accountant sign-off, or drop row 4 and run three rows.
8. **Never quote cycle time, rack count or glasses per hour.** Specs live on the product page and vary by unit.
- **Payoff tension, worth testing:** "$3.2K" is the smallest figure in the family, so the strike-through payoff lands softer here than it does at $30K. **Variant B:** replace the payoff line with *"Fixed before the weekend."* and keep everything else. That trades family consistency for urgency and may well beat the control on this tier. Run both.
- **Shorter-cut trim (8s):** cuts 1, 3, 5, 9, 11. VO: "Same glasswasher. Thirty-two hundred today, or four sixty-six a day. Clean glasses through a Friday. Get our latest stock list today."
