### ⚠ Claim checks — confirm before publishing

1. **Condition — the material one.** Every one of the 76 oven records in the Meta product feed carries `condition: "new"`, but brand guidance says most stock is refurbished-with-warranty. The 0:19 line assumes refurbished. **Either** reconcile the feed, **or** swap that beat to the neutral line: *"Premium brands, with warranty. Installed and serviced by Washpro."* Do not run "refurbished" over stock the feed calls new.
2. **GST basis.** Catalogue prices come through as plain NZD. Confirm they are GST-exclusive before "$20,000 + GST" goes to air; if they are GST-inclusive, the card reads **$20,000 incl. GST**.
3. **No daily figure used here, deliberately.** Brand reference approves "$4.66/day"; the live account is running "$3.99/day (A)" and "$6.99/day (B)". Three different numbers. Premium gear should lead on capital preservation regardless — but settle which daily figure is signed off before it appears anywhere.
4. **Price is a snapshot** — pulled from the Meta product catalog on 4 Sep 2026. Re-verify $20,000 against the live product page before the ad goes live.
