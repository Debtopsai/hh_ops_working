## Notes

- **Why the two captions differ:** Instagram uses the **Comment BROCHURE** auto-DM trigger (only works once the Meta/ManyChat auto-reply is set up). Facebook is link-friendly and the audience tolerates more text, so it gets the **direct stock-list link** and a lighter hashtag set. If run as a paid FB ad, put the link in the primary text and set the button to Learn More pointing at the stock list.
- **Compliance:** no price appears on screen, so no "+GST" is needed. Keep "Subject to credit approval" on the end card and in both captions. Approvals are never implied guaranteed.
- **Sustainability claims are kept truthful and soft** so they stay inside NZ Commerce Commission green-claims guidance. A real number (machines kept from landfill in a year, or a typical energy or water saving on an upgrade) lands harder and is more defensible than a general claim.
- **Refurb stays low-key** ("recondition and redeploy") per the brand rule, since sustainability is the one place it is a clear virtue.
