# 03 — Video Scripts (the two operator videos)

*These two scripts are the operator's — **canonical and fixed.** This file documents them and gives each a light shot treatment so they can be built/finished consistently (person-free motion graphics + real catalogue equipment), plus the two on-screen compliance fixes. The **ad copy that runs with each video is in `02`.***

**Format:** 9:16 (1080×1920) primary; 4:5 (1080×1350) for Feed. Watched **muted** — burned-in captions, on-screen text carries the message. **End-card CTA: "Get our latest stock list today"** (Learn More button → the stock list / brochure). Real equipment only; no Washpro on screen; "+GST" on any price; approval never implied guaranteed.

## VIDEO 2 — Speed

**Script (verbatim):** *"Monday, apply. Tuesday, approved. Thursday, delivered. Friday, equipment starts to pay for itself. Get our latest stock list today."*

| t | VO (the script) | On-screen text | Visual | Build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | "Monday, apply." | **MON** · *Apply — 5 minutes* | Phone/app apply motion on dark steel | Motion graphics |
| 2.0–4.0 | "Tuesday, approved." *(see fix)* | **TUE** · *A decision* · ⁎ *illustrative · subject to credit approval* | A step ticks / stamp settles (flame) | Motion graphics |
| 4.0–6.0 | "Thursday, delivered." | **THU** · *Delivered* · ⁎ *after your deposit* | Machine slides in on a van/plinth (real catalogue still) | MG over catalogue still |
| 6.0–8.0 | "Friday, equipment starts to pay for itself." | **FRI** · *It pays for itself* | Equipment working in a kitchen scene | MG over catalogue still |
| 8.0–11.0 | "Get our latest stock list today." | **Get our latest stock list today** · *Illustrative. Subject to credit approval. Deposit applies.* | End card: wordmark + Learn More pill + hirehospo.com | Motion graphics |

**On-screen compliance (needs sign-off — this is the plan's biggest Fair Trading exposure, `00` §3d):**
- The day-by-day timeline implies **guaranteed/automatic approval** and omits the **deposit**. Fine print in the ad copy can't cure a misleading *video*, so the video itself needs a light touch:
  - **Persistent super:** *"Illustrative. Subject to credit approval. Deposit applies."*
  - **Ideally soften one word:** *"Tuesday, **a decision**"* instead of *"Tuesday, approved."* (Keeps the rhythm; removes the guaranteed-approval implication.)
- The paired ad copy (`02` Ad 2) then carries the true, specific claim: *"most applications under $25,000 approved within 3 business days."*
