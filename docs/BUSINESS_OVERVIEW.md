# HireHospo — Business Overview

*Reference document for ops and email-drafting work. Compiled 19 July 2026 from
hirehospo.com / washpro.co.nz content and internal HireHospo knowledge bases.*

## The Business in One Line

HireHospo is a New Zealand credit-led finance provider for commercial kitchen
equipment. Washpro sources, refurbishes, delivers and services the equipment;
HireHospo wraps it in finance — turning a $4,000 dishwasher into a low weekly
payment so a hospitality operator can preserve cashflow.

## Vision, Mission & Value Proposition

- **Vision:** Fund hospitality dreams — remove the capital barrier that stops
  NZ hospitality operators from getting the equipment their business needs.
- **Mission:** Make premium commercial kitchen equipment accessible through
  fast approvals and low weekly payments, backed by local NZ delivery,
  installation and servicing.
- **Value proposition:** *Premium kitchen equipment, refurbished and warranted,
  on low weekly payments.*
  - Low weekly payments (always quoted **+ GST**), no large capital outlay
  - Fast approvals (24–48 hours; most applications under $25k approved within
    3 business days)
  - Funding up to **$50,000**, ~3-minute application
  - Reduced upfront deposit available for qualifying customers
  - Equipment upgrade path at end of term
  - Local support via Washpro (NZ-based delivery, install, service, priority
    maintenance on financed equipment)
  - 3,000+ NZ hospitality businesses served

## Finance Products

| Product | Term | Ownership | Best suited to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rent** | 12 months | No ownership included; end-of-term options: purchase at discount, continue at reduced rate, or upgrade | Startups or businesses not ready to commit to ownership |
| **Lease-to-Own** | 36 months | Customer owns the equipment at end of term; fixed weekly payments | Businesses with ≥1 year operating history wanting small weekly payments and a path to ownership |

### Deposit structures
- **Standard:** 10 weeks rent in advance + 10 weeks security deposit (20 weeks upfront)
- **Alternatives (case-by-case by credit tier):** 6+6, 8+8, 4+3 (special cases)
- Website reference point: 10% deposit of total equipment cost

### Additional costs
- Delivery and installation quoted separately; LPG conversion if required
- Delivery: 1–3 business days after deposit payment received

### Revenue model (internal context)
Margin on equipment (~30%), weekly recurring payments, late fees (10%),
admin fees ($25 + GST), end-of-term purchase options. The goal is a strong
recurring-revenue portfolio, not one-off wins.

### Golden rule
**Never send a quote without credit approval first** (credit check via
Checkmate). If declined, no quote is issued — politely.

## Equipment Catalogue (via Washpro)

One catalogue synced from washpro.co.nz to hirehospo.com — ~241 active
products, ~30 brands (in-house Starline through premium European: Rational,
Electrolux, Convotherm, Turbofan), active price range ~$795–$32,995 (median
~$3,600). Mostly **refurbished with warranty** — a selling point, not a
caveat: it keeps the weekly payment low, gives access to premium brands, and
the warranty removes the second-hand risk.

13 categories: commercial dishwashers, glasswashers, combi ovens, convection
ovens, ranges/cooktops/gas burners, griddles/grills/salamanders, deep
fryers/pasta cookers, pizza & conveyor ovens, bakery & dough equipment,
holding/display/food warming, refrigeration & ice, food prep & slicing,
other/specialty.

## The Washpro Partnership

- **HireHospo:** finance, contracts, payments, customer relationship
- **Washpro:** equipment sourcing, refurbishment, delivery, installation,
  maintenance, repairs (24/7 repair booking)
- HireHospo holds **zero inventory** — 100% of equipment access is through
  Washpro. Shared ownership/alignment, but the customer experience remains
  HireHospo's responsibility: if delivery is late, the customer blames
  HireHospo, so Washpro is managed proactively.
- Repairs/maintenance queries are directed to Washpro.

## Industries & Customers Served

Hospitality and food-service businesses across NZ: cafes, restaurants,
caterers, cloud/ghost kitchens, food trucks, bakeries, bars/pubs, plus retail
food businesses. Both startups (Rent) and established operators
(Lease-to-Own).

## Sales Flow

Lead (Washpro site, Meta/Google ads, direct, referral) → details collected →
**credit check (Checkmate)** → approved → quote (weekly + GST, deposit
breakdown, rent-vs-lease comparison, product links) sent via HTML email →
objections handled → proposal via Plutio → contract signed → deposit paid →
direct debit set up (GoCardless) → dispatch triggered (Washpro fulfils,
1–3 business days).

## Brand Tone (baseline for email drafting)

Clear, confident, structured — underwriting, not selling. Transparent about
costs, terms and obligations. Professional finance provider, not a discount
shop. Sell the **payment, not the price**; never state a specific weekly
figure before credit approval. NZ-local voice and support are core to the
brand.

## Brand Typography

Confirmed from hirehospo.com (Aug 2026). Use these two typefaces in all
customer-facing assets (emails, landing pages, ad frames):

- **Headlines:** Merriweather (serif, bold); fallback Georgia, serif
- **Body & labels:** Inter; fallback Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif

These are the only two brand typefaces. Earlier drafts used Space Grotesk and a
monospace face provisionally; those are retired. Avoid em dashes in
customer-facing copy.
