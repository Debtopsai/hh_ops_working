# Audio brief — HireHospo "Twenty grand, for one oven" — 15s

## Music brief
- Genre/feel: warm, capable, mid-tempo — understated groove or warm electronic. No vocals, no EDM drop, no corporate-inspirational piano, no discount-shop stings.
- BPM ~96, steady. Feel: a well-run kitchen after service — competent, calm, a little warmth. The bed should sound like a lender who doesn't need the deal: assured, unhurried.
- Key/tone: minor-leaning, restrained on the problem beats; opens to a warmer major colour at the bridge as the wordmark resolves.
- Energy curve by beat: **restrained on the hook (0:00–0:03) → dip / thin out on the capital-hit drain (0:03–0:06, pull low-end and pads, let ambience show) → lift at the bridge (0:06, add warmth + pulse as the timeline ticks) → settle as the ledger rows tick and APPROVED lands → resolve with a short warm tail under the end card (0:13–0:15).** No build to a drop — the lift is a *steadying*, not a payoff.

## VO direction — credit-led operator
- Voice: clear, confident, structured. NZ accent. ~2.4–2.6 wps. Zero hype, zero upsell energy — a lender talking to an operator who knows their numbers. Clean breath cuts; let the hook and the bridge breathe.

| Time | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | "Twenty thousand dollars. For one oven." | Flat, factual. Small, dry stress on "twenty thousand". Full stop of silence after "oven" — let the number sit. Not incredulous; stating a fact the operator already fears. |
| 0:03–0:06 | "Pay cash — it's gone before service." | Lower, quieter. The cost, said plainly. Slight fall on "gone". No drama — this is the calm that makes it land. |
| 0:06–0:08 | "Or finance it with HireHospo." | The turn. Warmer, a touch more forward. Even weight on "HireHospo" — brand said cleanly, never sold. |
| 0:08–0:10.5 | "The same oven — backed by warranty." | Confident, matter-of-fact. "Backed by warranty" said as a strength, no apology, tiny lift on "warranty". |
| 0:10.5–0:13 | "The oven works. Your capital keeps working too." | The capital-preservation pair — the emotional centre. Steady, assured. Light stress on both "works" / "working". |
| 0:13–0:15 | "Apply now — approved in 24 to 48 hours." | Crisp, low-pressure invitation. No urgency, no push. "24 to 48 hours" even and factual. |

- Record 2–3 reads of the hook and the CTA line at varying warmth for the editor to choose. Prefer the drier hook and the warmer CTA.

## Ducking & loudness
Music −6 to −9 dB under VO (the hook may ride without a full duck since VO is sparse there); SFX peaks duck a further 3–6 dB so nothing masks the VO. VO is the priority track throughout. Master **−14 LUFS integrated, −1.0 dBTP**. Kitchen ambience stays textural, never a literal foley bed.
