# Lead auto-reply, copy variations (personal email from Urman)

Four human, first-person variations for the Facebook lead auto-reply. Same
intent in each: Urman introduces himself, says he'll be in touch soon (within a
day), asks the lead to check the brochure and note the machines they want, and
keeps pricing off the table until the call. Pick one as the primary; the others
work as A/B tests.

Personalisation: `{{first_name}}` in the greeting (set a fallback of `there`).
Everything else is fixed. Links show as plain URLs in the plain-text send and as
clickable links in HTML.

> None of these quote a price or a payment figure (the golden rule: no quote
> before credit approval). They also omit a formal finance disclaimer to keep
> the personal feel. If your deliverability owner wants one, add a light
> "subject to credit approval; normal lending criteria apply" line to Urman's
> email signature rather than the body.
