Ongoing local support, maintenance plans and technician services mean you are never on your own in the heat of service. When something needs attention, we are a phone call away, right here in Auckland.

## Our sustainability mission: good equipment, longer lives

We think the most sustainable commercial kitchen is one that gets the most out of every machine. That belief runs through how we work.

- **We give quality equipment a longer life.** A well-built combi oven or dishwasher is made to run for years. By reconditioning and redeploying quality machines to the operators who need them, we keep good equipment working and out of landfill.
- **We help you right-size, not over-buy.** Finance lets you get exactly the equipment your kitchen needs today, rather than over-committing to machines you will not use. Less waste, less idle capital.
- **We help you upgrade to efficient equipment.** Modern commercial ovens, fridges and dishwashers use far less energy and water than older units. Making that upgrade affordable is good for your power bill and good for the planet.
- **We keep machines running with local servicing.** Maintenance and technician support extend the working life of your equipment, which is the simplest sustainability win there is: a machine that keeps running is a machine that never needs replacing.

Our mission is a hospitality industry that grows without waste, where great equipment is used well, looked after, and passed on to its next kitchen when the time is right.
